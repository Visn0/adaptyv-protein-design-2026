# Pristine batch results: raw generation passes the gate, but not specificity

**Headline: 100 raw designs from the frozen RFD4-Proteina produced 9 organizer-gate
passers and one design (pr-089) that beats the champion on the gate metric. None
of them beats the champions on the criterion that decides the challenge —
discrimination against the WT peptide. The submission does not change.**

## What was run

100 designs generated by the frozen (released EMA) RFD4-Proteina against
`target_pmhc_PEPRIM_annotated.cif`, folded with Boltz (5 diffusion samples each),
scored with the campaign's s1 instrument. The 9 gate passers were then folded in
the WT and peptide-free states, and the top 3 were folded across 3 seeds.

All local. Zero cloud spend.

| pr-024 | -0.06 | 1 | 0 |
| pr-043 | +0.58 | 0 | 0 |

Every new design reads -0.28 or higher against -2.60 to -3.75 for the submitted
champions, and no new design places a histidine in the Asp6 shell. Three
independent instruments — the gate read, the WT discrimination, and the
E-score — agree that the new batch does not beat the champions.

## Conclusions

1. **Specificity is produced by the evolution loop, not by the generator.** Raw
   RFD4-Proteina output yields a strong interface and poor peptide
   discrimination. The 20-generation selection loop is what drove WT binding to
   near zero. The submitted set stays as it is.
2. **The gate read alone is not a sufficient filter.** pr-089 would have entered
   the submission on the primary metric and failed the challenge on the second
   criterion. Every candidate must pass the WT discrimination before it earns a
   slot.
3. **The free-state read needs care.** With no peptide chain present,
   `peptide_read` is 0 by construction, so it carries no information. The usable
   free-state signal is `hla_read`: the champions and the new specific designs
   both fall to 0.000-0.237 against roughly 0.9 in the bound state, which
   confirms they do not bind peptide-free MHC.
4. **Raw generation is a useful candidate source.** 9 percent of designs pass
   the gate, so the generator plus the funnel is a working front end for the
   evolution loop — it just is not a substitute for it.
