# Deep dive: physical design details & numbers from published de novo pMHC-binder campaigns and the public Competition-5 analyses

Compiled 2026-10-30 (UTC+1) for Challenge 5 (KRAS G12D / HLA-A*11:01). Everything below is from primary sources fetched today; every claim carries a URL. Numbers are verbatim from the sources unless explicitly marked as my own computation. Items I could not fetch are labelled in the Gaps section.

**Critical construct note (verified by me directly from the PDBs):** the challenge-provided structure **9UV8** contains a **9-mer peptide `VVGADGVGK` with Asp at position 5** (chain C, residues 1–9: V-V-G-A-D-G-V-G-K; I downloaded and counted). PDB **8I5E** (WT off-target) contains `VVGAGGVGK` (Gly at p5). rpinho's C5 docs instead describe a **10-mer `VVVGADGVGK` with Asp at position 6**, built from PDB 7OW6 (which does contain the 10-mer). So "which residue is the specificity handle" is frame-dependent: **p5 in the 9UV8/8I5E frame, p6 in the 7OW6/10-mer frame**. ElainW's repos used 9UV8 as provided (9-mer, "mutant Asp12 (p5) at the apex"). Reconcile before locking hotspot residue numbers.

## 1. Liu et al. 2025 — "Design of high-specificity binders for peptide–MHC-I complexes" (Science 389:386-391, adv0185; preprint bioRxiv 2024.11.28.625793)

URLs: https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.adv0185 · https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13077772/ (Science version, free PMC) · https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11623666/ (preprint) · code: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15169815 · repo: https://github.com/77forest/pMHCI_binder_design

**Design geometry ("arc above the groove")**
- RFdiffusion was "targeted to the outward-facing residues of the bound peptide" — i.e. **upward-facing peptide residues (out of the MHC groove) were the hotspots**; binders "arc above the central peptide binding groove" and "make extensive contacts with the peptide" with "limited contact with the MHC". Preprint wording: "we used the generative AI protein design method RFdiffusion to generate protein backbones specifying the upward facing (out of the MHC groove) residues of the peptide as hotspots".
- Scaffold selection: backbones hosting side chains making many peptide contacts were kept by **Rosetta contact molecular surface (CMS)**, restricted to the peptide (or individual peptide residues) vs the whole binder surface; cutoffs determined empirically per target.
- After the first targets, **partial diffusion** (partial_t between **12 and 25** of 50 total denoising steps) was used to adapt privileged scaffolds to new pMHC targets ("partial diffusion carries out partial noising-denoising to sample around an input structure").
- Methods parameters: **1,000–20,000 backbones per RFdiffusion cycle; 4–32 MPNN sequences per backbone**; output screening on AF2 initial-guess (pAE interaction, binder pLDDT, binder RMSD), AF2 monomer pLDDT, Rosetta CMS per target residue/range, and ProteinMPNN log-probability of designs vs alanine-scan peptide mutants. Cutoffs varied per target and per round; the design process was iterated through new partial-diffusion rounds.

**How many designs / how many targets**
- **11 pMHC targets** (Science version; 10 in the preprint), HLA alleles A*01:01, A*02:01, A*03:01, C*07:02, presenting 9- and 10-mer peptides from viral proteins, TAAs, and neoantigens.
- **8 of 11** targets via de novo diffusion, **3** via partial diffusion; "oligonucleotide pools encoding between **200 and 12,000 designs**" per target, displayed on yeast.
- Yeast screen: FACS selecting on-target tetramer (PE) binding with no 1–4 closely related off-target tetramers (APC) on the same HLA ("staining in the lower-right quadrant indicates specific on-target binding"; flow at **10 nM tetramer**), followed by **NGS enrichment analysis or clonal selection** (Illumina NextSeq/MiSeq; all naive and sorted pools sequenced).
- Structures: AF3/Chai-1 predictions matched design models (table S2); a **2.2-Å crystal structure of mart1-3** with MART-1 pMHCI matched the design model at **Cα RMSD = 0.4 Å** and **interface all-atom RMSD = 0.4 Å across 15 interface residues** (PDB 9O5S).
- SPR: hiv-10 and mage-513 bind cognate pMHC with "single- to double-digit nanomolar" affinities; PRAME binders prame-9 and prame-2: **K_D 96 nM and 35 nM** (designed from AF3-predicted structures).
- CAR/Jurkat: **10–30 CAR constructs per design**; designs for **8 targets** conferred peptide-specific T-cell activation; primary T-cell killing demonstrated for PRAME and WT1 binders on peptide-pulsed T2 cells.

**Specificity filters (two computational approaches)**
1. **ProteinMPNN alanine-scan filter**: ProteinMPNN scores the likelihood of each peptide residue when in complex with the binder; keep designs where the on-target residue has high probability and substitutions reduce it considerably (filter on log-prob of the on-target residue or on the on-target − alanine difference).
2. **Fine-tuned AF2 specificity filter ("AF-MHC screening")**: they took the **AlphaFold2 fine-tuned for MHC (Motmaen 2023)** and "altered [it] to predict minibinder-pMHC complexes through additional templating of the minibinder". For each design they predicted the complex with the on-target and **2–3 relevant off-target peptides in the same HLA allele**, computed **pAE interaction between only the peptide and the minibinder**, and filtered on **delta pAE interaction (on-target − off-target; more negative is better), with scores ranging from −10 to −0.5 depending on the on-target/off-target pair**.
- Off-target peptide identification: selective Cross-Reactive Antigen Presentation outputs cross-checked with **NetMHC4.1 (EL_rank ≤ 0.5)**, then a "simple blossom alignment" against the peptide of interest to rank the top off-targets.

**D29 "gatekeeper" clash mechanism (mage-513)**
- In the design model, "D29 of the design is close to P4 of the peptide without making a clear interaction"; concluded "this residue likely played a gatekeeper role by clashing with off-target peptides with bulky residues at site 4".
- Test: "Mutation of D29 does not influence Jurkat activation upon pulsing with MAGE peptide but **did have higher background activation when coincubated with HLA-A*01:01–expressing 293T cells**, likely because of increased cross-reactivity, consistent with a gatekeeper role".
- Key contacts of mage-513: H-bonds to H7 side chain and L8 backbone; hydrophobic contacts via design L37 and L86 to peptide L8; H-bond R33(design)–N66(HLA). Alanine mutation of peptide I5/H7/L8 or binder L37/D40/L86 reduces activation.

**Extent of MHC contacts**
- The design premise is "pMHC interaction interfaces focused on the presented peptide with limited contact with the MHC". Quantitatively (not in the main text): the ElainW analysis of the 18–19 validated designs (data S1 + AF3 models) measures **ΔSASA 2100–3300 Å² (median ~2900)** for the whole interface with **7–14 binder residues at the peptide interface**, contacting **2–5 peptide positions including the mutation-bearing residue**; validated binders are 59–106 aa α-helical miniproteins. (Analysis by ElainW from paper data: https://github.com/ElainW/agent_pmhc_kras_binder_2/blob/main/docs/02_methods_and_inputs.md)

**Yeast cross-reactivity screening procedure (MAGE-513)**
- A comprehensive library of **covalently linked peptide–HLA-A*01:01 complexes displayed on yeast** (Birnbaum 2014 design; P3 = D/E and P9 = Y anchors fixed, NNK at other positions) was probed with purified, biotinylated binder; streptavidin magnetic-bead selections; top hits re-tested by peptide pulsing of 293T cells + mage-513 CAR-Jurkat CD69 assay. Of the top 3 activating peptides, 2 shared the outward-facing side chains I5/H7/L8 with MAGE-A3. A second, sequence-similarity scan of the human proteome surfaced **EVDPIGHVY and VTDFISHLF** as the most activating — again sharing I5/H7/L8. Conclusion: cross-reactivity is confined to peptides with similar outward-facing residues — "predictable range".

## 2. Johansen et al. 2025 — "De novo-designed pMHC binders facilitate T cell–mediated cytotoxicity toward cancer cells" (Science 389:380-385, adv0422; preprint bioRxiv 2024.11.27.624796)

URLs: https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.adv0422 · https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2024.11.27.624796v1 (open preprint) · PDF with numbers used below: https://bookcafe.yuntsg.com/ueditor/jsp/upload/file/20250822/1755828132710085362.pdf · PDB https://www.rcsb.org/structure/9NNF · EMDB https://www.ebi.ac.uk/emdb/EMD-49573

**NY1-B04 essentials**
- Target SLLMWITQC/HLA-A*02:01 (NY-ESO-1 157-165); design input crystal structure **PDB 2BNR**; **5,500 RFdiffusion designs**, with **all peptide residues as hotspots** ("all amino acids from the peptide serving as hotspots to increase the chance of binding being primarily driven by the peptide rather than the MHC"); 4 sequences per backbone → **109** selected → MPNN/AF2 reinterpretation (100 sequences/design) → **44** tested.
- In silico cross-panning: 9 single/double-mutant variants + 1 quadruple mutant modelled with AF3; keep binders with **iPAE < 10 Å on-target and > 10 Å on the variants**; NY1-B04 had the highest predicted specificity; 484 structures total (11 peptides × 44 binders).
- Affinity: **K_D = 6.92 nM (SD 0.7 nM)** by BLI (6xHis-tagged NY1-B04 on HIS1K tips; 1:1 fit; twofold dilutions from 1 µM) — the Science version states 6.9 nM. Enrichment: **>2 log2FC** in sorted vs pre-sort cells.
- Cryo-EM: **3.8-Å map** of NY1-B04/SLLMWITQC/HLA-A*02:01 (PDB **9NNF**), confirming the AF2-predicted binding mode; binder construct 145 aa (141 resolved in 9NNF).

**9NNF interface contacts (computed by me directly from the deposited coordinates, heavy-atom ≤ 4.5 Å)**
- **Peptide positions contacted: P2, P3, P4, P5, P6, P7, P8** (21 contact pairs). Binder residues contacting peptide: 93, 96, 97, 100, 103, 104, 107, 115, 118, 119, 122, 123, 126, 127, 130.
- **M4 (P4) pocket**: M93, A97, A100*, F123, L127, A130 — matches the paper's stated pocket residues **M93, A97, A100, F123, L127, A130** ("NY1-B04 encloses position M4 and W5 in a combined hydrophobic pocket"). W5 (P5) is packed by A100, S103, M104, Y107, Q119, V122, F123.
- Key polar contacts (N/O ≤ 3.5 Å): **E96 OE2 – M4 backbone N, 3.07 Å**; **Q119 OE1 – W5 NE1, 2.97 Å**.
- **MHC contacts**: 46 contact pairs ≤ 4.5 Å, all to the HLA heavy chain (**zero to β2m**): **α1 helix** at standard positions 58 (E), 59 (Y), 62 (G), 65 (R), 69 (A), 72 (Q), 73 (T), 76 (V) and **α2 helix** at 149 (A), 150 (A), 151 (H), 152 (V), 154 (E), 155 (Q), 158 (A), 159 (Y), 162 (G), 163 (T), 166 (E), 167 (W), 170 (R). (Deposited chain-B numbering minus 145 = standard HLA numbering; e.g. A139↔E58, T133↔E166, E140↔R170.) So the binder makes **more MHC-helix contacts (46 pairs) than peptide contacts (21 pairs)** at this distance cutoff — the "peptide-focused" binder is still MHC-heavy in raw contact counts.
- Consistency check with the paper's MD: the paper names **Glu11, Glu96, Thr133, Arg139, Glu140** as crucial HLA-A*02:01 partners (my contacts include E96, T133, R139, E140) and **Tyr107, Glu115, Gln119 (+ "Lys212")** as key peptide-stabilising residues (my contacts: Y107, E115, Q118, Q119). Note "Lys212" is outside the 145-aa binder — likely a numbering artifact in the paper; treat as-is.

**Mutation tolerance (which positions carry specificity)**
- NY1-B04 bound the single mutants **SLLAWITQC (M4A), SLLMYITQC (W5Y), SLLMWITQV (C9V)**; M4A and C9V were ranked with low iPAE (<10 Å) in the in-silico cross-panning; it also bound **SLLEHITQC (W5E/I6H)** to some extent but **did not bind SLEWITQC (W5E)**.
- "Complete abrogation of NY1-B04 binding when introducing polar, negatively charged amino acids (aspartic acid and glutamic acid) in position 4" (M4E, M4D; also M4R, W5R repelled by the hydrophobic pocket). Cross-panning accuracy: **19/27 correct binding/non-binding calls** (fig. S7C).
- Interpretation: specificity is carried by the **combined hydrophobic pocket that encloses M4 + W5** (peptide positions 4–5, the TCR-proximal central bulge) plus contacts reaching C9; anchor-adjacent positions are not the specificity determinants. Tolerated: small/aromatic-neutral swaps at 4/5 and C9; intolerant: polar/charged residues in that pocket. This mirrors the KRAS challenge: bury the mutation-bearing side chain in a complementary pocket and repel the WT chemistry.

**MD-based success prediction**
- MD descriptors: **conformational diversity** measured by interface B-factor, **electrostatic interaction-energy fluctuations**, and **van der Waals interaction-energy fluctuations**, considering salt bridges/H-bonds that persist **>10% of the simulation**. NY1-B04 showed "the lowest conformational flexibility in the binding interface and the lowest interaction energy fluctuations" — predicted the most promising of the 44; it was the only experimentally enriched hit. AF2 predictions took "<10 sec per miBd-target complex" and "correlated substantially (**89%**) with in vitro specificity screening" (cross-panning accuracy N=9 = 8/9).
- The follow-up meta-analysis of this group (Overath et al., 3,766 binders; AF3 **ipSAE** as best predictor) is in §6.

**Second target: RVTDESILSY/HLA-A*01:01**
- Neoantigen from AKAP9 P1796L (metastatic melanoma, "private" passenger mutation); **no experimental structure** → complex modelled with AF2, "most confident structure (pLDDT 95.7)" used; miBds designed as before; **95 miBds** tested by lentiviral mammalian display; tetramer+ library gMFI **18,546 vs 14,103** for primary T cells with CRISPR knock-in of the patient's RVTDESILSY-specific TCR. Sequencing of sorted cells identified **two unique binders: SILSY1-B11 and SILSY1-G05 — success rate ~2%** (log2FC > 2). (Independently confirmed as binders in the InstaNexus paper: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13084398/.)
- BIKE format: NY1-B04 as CAR recognition domain ("de novo Binders for Immune-mediated Killing Engagers"); co-culture with mCherry+ A375 melanoma at 0.5:1 and 1:1, Incucyte imaging every 4 h for 72 h — "selectively and effectively killed NY-ESO-1+ melanoma cells compared to non-transduced controls".

## 3. Odin-Multi — "Specificity-driven protein binder design with Odin-Multi" (bioRxiv 2026, DOI 10.64898/2026.09.08.749745, posted 2026-09-09; Brasas, Christensen, Björnsson, …, Johansen, Jenkins)

URLs: https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.08.749745v1 · code https://github.com/DigBioLab/odin_multi · data https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22292558 · (numbers below extracted from the full PDF text, https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.08.749745v1.full.pdf)

**How the repulsive off-target objective works**
- Framework: JAX, extends **ColabDesign + BindCraft**; in each iteration **frozen AlphaFold2 predicts the same evolving binder sequence in parallel against every on-target and off-target**; on-target losses are attractive (iPAE, iPTM, contacts, pLDDT, compactness, ProteinMPNN/LigandMPNN compatibility); off-target losses are repulsive; gradients are merged with **PCGrad** (RMS-normalised, context-weighted). Sequence state is a shared 20×m logit tensor; three-stage optimisation: **75 logits-to-soft; 45 temperature-annealed softmax; 20 straight-through one-hot** (pMHC/toxin campaigns).
- pMHC off-target loss specifics: **clipped normalised iPAE** (raw iPAE ÷ 31 Å; loss active only below 0.35) and **iPTM loss active only above 0.45**; an **off-target hotspot-contact loss** penalises predicted binder contact probability > 0.30 with off-target hotspot residues (20 Å cutoff, weight 0.05). Experimental campaigns: **M4A off-target weight 0.30 (repulsive) + empty HLA-A*02:01 weight 0.01**; CMV condition used CMV pMHC weight 0.20 + empty HLA 0.01.
- Candidate ranking: Scross = max_t iPAE_t (worst on-target) for cross-reactivity; **Sspec = min_o[min(iPAE_o,15)] / max_t iPAE_t** for specificity; experimental pMHC selection required **off-target-to-target iPAE ratio > 1.5, ipSAE > 0.45, shape complementarity > 0.5** (toxin campaign: ipSAE > 0.61).

**The 6.0% → 14.2% numbers (pMHC specificity benchmark)**
- On-target SLLMWITQC/HLA-A*02:01 vs off-target **SLLAWITQC (M4A)** — "99.6% overall sequence identity; Cα RMSD 0.23 Å". 400 designs per condition.
- Criterion: target iPTM > 0.5 AND target/off-target iPTM ratio > 2.5: **24/400 (6.0%) without counter-selection vs 57/400 (14.2%) with the off-target objective**. Primary comparison: permutation test over **160 ratio thresholds from 1 to 5**, maximum absolute between-condition difference **13.50 percentage points, Holm-adjusted P = 0.00070**. Independent AF3 re-evaluation: **38/400 (9.5%) → 59/400 (14.8%)**, max diff 7.50 pp, Holm-adjusted **P = 0.0612**.
- Other benchmarks: GPCR pairs **83.5–96.8%** (joint) vs **6.8–36.3%** (single-target) at iPTM > 0.5; toxins **9.2%** (joint) vs **0.8%** (single-target).

**Experimental pMHC campaign (mammalian display)**
- Three conditions: Group 1 target-only (203 trajectories, 155 yielded); Group 2 CMV NLVPMVATV + empty-HLA counter-selection (453 run, 341 yielded); Group 3 **M4A + empty-HLA counter-selection (1,700 run, 1,235 yielded)**; early-termination rates 24/25/27%. Pooled library of **500 miBds (166/167/167)** in TCR-deficient Jurkat; FACS tetramer+/-; top 20 by log2FC (lowest selected 1.2): **11/166, 6/167, 3/167**.
- Outcomes: Group 1 all 11 clones bound, N1/N3/N4 exceeded the 1G4 TCR clone in MFI, but **N1 and N3 (highest avidity) also bound M4A with comparably high avidity**; Group 2 mostly low-avidity binders; Group 3 all retained target binding with **S1 and S2 exceeding both 1G4 and NY1-B04 in MFI**; **S2 discriminated best** (target/M4A log2 MFI ratio); S1/S3 only modest. CMV cross-reactivity: only clone N8 (Group 1), none in counter-selected groups.
- **Geometry of the improved design (S2)**: in the AF3-predicted complex, "the peptide **Met4 side chain occupies a pocket on the minibinder surface**, with predicted contacts within **4.2 Å** involving **Met20, Val23, the Met54 backbone carbonyl, Phe57, Glu58, and Trp61**". Rationale: Met→Ala shrinks packing in that pocket → lower M4A staining. Explicit caveat: based on the predicted structure only.
- Yield cost of counter-selection: enrichment-threshold pass rate 11/166 → 6/167 (CMV) and 3/167 (M4A); Groups 2/3 differ in both off-target identity and weight, so their comparison does not isolate either factor.
- Toxin lead (context): Poly 5, BLI KD1/KD2 = **11.95/48.41 nM** (Erabutoxin A) and **34.43/77.49 nM** (N. kaouthia NK-shNTx fraction 3), 2:1 heterogeneous-ligand model; hit rate **1/30 (3.3%)** or 1/24 (4.2%) among expressers.

## 4. ADAPT — "Targeting peptide–MHC complexes with designed T cell receptors and antibodies" (bioRxiv 2025.11.19.689381, posted 2025-11-20; Motmaen, Jude, Wang, …, Garcia, Baker, Bradley)

URLs: https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.11.19.689381v1 · https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12667852/ · code https://github.com/phbradley/ADAPT (MIT, 22 stars, created 2025-10-30, last pushed 2026-09-03) · params/data https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17488258

**What it designs:** TCRs **and** TCR-mimic antibodies (not minibinders). Pipeline: a version of **AlphaFold2 fine-tuned on TCR:pMHC structures and binding data** ("alphafold_finetune" approach, Ref. Motmaen 2023 lineage) does docking, with **four generic TCR-like docking templates** (covering observed native docking modes, forward/reverse for antibodies) + **random CDR3 sequences from a database of 1,688,863 paired human TCR sequences**; then **ProteinMPNN** designs the 6 CDR loops (temperature 0.1, model v_48_020, 3 seqs); then **redock with AF2**; design quality evaluated with AF2 metrics + a **RoseTTAFold2 fine-tuned on antibodies/TCRs**; top 100–500 designs/target refined by a genetic algorithm alternating MPNN design and AF2 redocking. Only **two AF2 forward passes per design**; ran on 11-GB commodity GPUs. (RFantibody parameter sets are distributed with the code; ADAPT itself was better at native-like docking than a fine-tuned RFdiffusion in their hands.)

**TCR results (Table 1):** 9 pMHC targets; binders for **6**: A01-EVD (MAGE-A3) 16.7% clonal; A02-TLM (PAP) 20.0% clonal; A02-GLM (SARS) 4.2% clonal; A02-LLW (YFV) 20.8% clonal; A02-ALY (TdT) 0.3% library (1000–5000 designs); B44-SEI (PIK3CA) 0.1% library. **No successful designs for A02-HMT (p53), A03-ALH (PIK3CA), and A11-VVV — KRAS G12D `VVVGADGVGK`/HLA-A*11:01: 0% (clonal).** The exact competition target was attempted with TCR-style design and failed in their hands.
**Antibody results (Table 2):** 3/3 targets succeeded in small screens — A01-EVD 6.25%, A02-TLM 8.33%, A02-ALY 5.21% clonal; monovalent affinities **5 nM–700 nM** (most high-nanomolar); cryo-EM of **vAB-30** (A01-EVD): full-complex Cα-RMSD **0.7 Å**, CDR **0.6 Å**; **vAB-66** (A02-TLM): 0.6 Å / 1.2 Å. vAB-30 peptide readout: HC CDR2 Arg54 H-bond to peptide Tyr9 main-chain O + LC CDR1 Tyr32 CH–π to Pro4; MHC contacts to α1 (CDR2/CDR3 HC) and α2 (CDR1/2/3 LC). vAB-66: HC CDR1 Asp33 H-bond to Ser4 + vdW from CDR3 HC; contacts to α1 and α2.
**Specificity findings (why cross-reactivity happens):** all-vs-all binding screen showed off-target binding "particularly to the A02-TLM pMHC"; many designed TCRs showed "relatively high levels of non-specific activation in the absence of target peptide"; "the more specific designs tend to have weaker on-target binding than non-specific designs"; binding vs activation correlated strongly (**Pearson r = 0.87, p < 10⁻⁸⁰**); "The specificity of nearly all of these in silico-generated sequences is likely insufficient for targeted killing of specific cell populations"; mutations at non-contacted peptide positions were better tolerated than at contacted positions.
**Status (as of 2026-10-30):** bioRxiv preprint only — PMID 41332722, PMC12667852 (NIH preprint pilot), no journal version found; code + fine-tuned AF2 and RFantibody parameters + CDR3 database on Zenodo; provisional patent 63/910,643 (Fred Hutch). One of their own successful antibody templates is PDB 7YV1 (a K-Ras binder scaffold) — not pMHC.

### 5a. rpinho/adaptyv-challenges — c5_prep (Ricardo Pinho; files dated 2026-10-27/28)
Repo: https://github.com/rpinho/adaptyv-challenges · APBS: https://github.com/rpinho/adaptyv-challenges/blob/main/c5_prep/C5_APBS_ELECTROSTATIC_DIFFERENCE_20261027.md · controls: .../C5_CONTROL_VALIDATION_RESULTS_20261027.md · positive control: .../C5_POSITIVE_CONTROL_20261027.md · panel: .../C5_SCORING_STRATEGY_PANEL_20261027.md · overnight: .../C5_OVERNIGHT_DESIGN_REPORT_20261028.md · retro: https://github.com/rpinho/adaptyv-challenges/blob/main/RETRO.md · compute: .../c5_prep/C5_COMPUTE_NOTES_20261027.md

**APBS electrostatic difference (state1 G12D 10-mer vs state2 in-silico D→G; PARSE, pH 7.4, 150 mM NaCl; 2,412 binder-shell points 4–12 Å from peptide):**
- Verdict **MEASURABLE**. Mean ΔΦ (G12D − WT) = **−0.26 kT/e** (−0.16 kcal/mol); std 0.71 kT/e; max |ΔΦ| = 30.4 kT/e near-surface (4-6 Å, PB artifact) but **8.4 kT/e (5.0 kcal/mol) at the realistic 6–8 Å binder distance**; 0.9 kT/e at 8-10 Å. Shell points > 0.1 kT/e: **80.1%**; > 0.5: 8.9%; > 1.0: 1.0%.
- Per-residue maxima: **P6 (Asp/Gly) 30.4** (25 pts), P7 3.5 (615 pts, broadest zone), P10 9.6, P2 8.4, P8 1.4, P9 1.0. Direction: consistently **negative** (G12D more negative) → "A binder placing positive charge (Arg, Lys, His) in the P6–P7 groove region would experience favorable electrostatics with G12D and lose that stabilization with WT."
- Design implications listed: place complementary positive charge **6–8 Å above P6-P7** (where TCRβ **His100** sits in the JDI TCR, **7.5 Å** from the Asp6 carboxylate); **hotspot RFdiffusion on HLA Arg114, Gln70, Gln156, Trp147** (groove microenvironment), **NOT on Asp6 directly** (buried, SASA = 0.00 Å²; side-chain SASA 8.8 Å² = 10.4% relative; salt-bridged to HLA Arg114); negative design against WT and peptide-free states is "built in".

**Hotspot corrections (the "Fable 5.1" structural measurement):**
- Asp6 carboxylate (OD2) SASA = **0.00 Å²**; side chain ~90% buried; already salt-bridged to HLA **Arg114** + contacts Gln70/Gln156/Trp147; nearest TCR atom to the carboxylate **7.5 Å** (contacts via TCRβ His100 to backbone only). "Design for direct Asp contacts is not an available strategy" (in the 7OW6/10-mer frame).
- BindCraft overnight run: hotspots v1 (buried groove floor A70/A114/A147/A156/C5-C8) → 0/1 passed stage 1; **v2 hotspots (accessible groove rim A69/A155/C7/C8) → 2/2 passed stage 1**. Final: **0/100+ MPNN designs accepted across 5 successful hallucinations**; best trajectory (pLDDT 0.88, i_pTM 0.88, 24 interface residues) still 0/20 MPNN pass — "AF2 predicts entirely different structures for MPNN-redesigned sequences on this multi-chain pMHC target"; multi-template detargeting hung >2 h on XLA compile; recommendations: standard `4stage` (not `4stage_duo`), softmax pLDDT threshold 0.65–0.70, more MPNN sequences, 60–80-aa binders, BindCraft2 on Tamarind, or RFdiffusion instead.

**Positive control (JDI TCR, PDB 7OW6; Poole et al. Nat Commun 2022, DOI 10.1038/s41467-022-32811-1):** parent TCR **K_D 63 µM for G12D**; **NB** (no binding) to WT, G12C, ERAS, RRAS, MRAS, DIRAS2, RAP1A, self-peptide pool. Specificity is "driven by indirect electrostatic interactions... NOT visible in static structures".

**Control validation: verdict FAIL.** ColabFold AF2-Multimer v3 (5 models, 3 recycles): ipTM(G12D) = ipTM(WT) = **0.84** at best model; means 0.818 / 0.772 / 0.710 (peptide-free); "AF2-Multimer ipTM alone cannot rank designs for G12D selectivity".

**Multi-model consistency filter (from RETRO, C4 origin):** within-design model variance mean **0.025 iPTM** > between-design SD **0.015 iPTM** — "iPTM ranking is noise"; working filter = **5-model mean iPTM ≥ 0.826 AND 5-model range ≤ 0.03**. Also: 66/342 C4 designs had sequence liabilities (His6 tags, homopolymer tracts, low entropy); C4 cost $1.83–2.79/design (≈$0.58/design for AF2m scoring at $0.12/model); **C5 estimate $1,100–1,700 for a 200-design funnel across the three states**; amortise target-side MSAs (heavy chain 281 aa, b2m 99 aa fixed; three target-side inputs for the whole challenge; sha256s given). Note: their compute notes describe the G12D peptide as **len 10** and build states from **7OW6** — see the construct note at the top.

### 5b. ElainW/agent_pmhc_kras_binder_2 (agentic KRAS-G12D campaign on 9UV8/8I5E)
Repo: https://github.com/ElainW/agent_pmhc_kras_binder_2 · methods: .../docs/02_methods_and_inputs.md · summary: .../docs/04_summary_report.md · log: .../docs/03_design_log.md · designs: .../designs/README.md

- Pipeline: RFdiffusion (backbones; **peptide-only hotspots B184+B186+B187**, explicit correction that HLA-helix hotspots bias toward the conserved helix surface) → ProteinMPNN (multi-seq) → ESMFold triage → `mpnn_spec_score` (ProteinMPNN conditional-prob alanine scan) → AF2 initial-guess (pose-templated) → custom **pMHC-fold 3-chain AF2-MHC-finetuned model** (self-templated, on/off ΔPAE) → **AF3 Server no-MSA** (independent; real MSA for MHC) → Rosetta InterfaceAnalyzer after FastRelax.
- **Gate table with cutoffs** (docs/02): `af2_pae_interaction < 5`; `binder_rmsd < 2 Å`; ESMFold pLDDT ≥ 80; `cms_Asp_p5` ≥ 2 contacts (target ≥ 4); `rosetta_ddG < −50 REU`; **`delta_pae_specificity` < 0 (on − off; more negative better)**; **`mpnn_spec_score` within-backbone ranking only — never a cross-backbone gate** (rule confirmed by paper author Bingxu Liu, 2026-06-23); AF3 gates `iptm ≥ 0.90` and binder–peptide `iptm ≥ 0.77`; ipSAE rank-only.
- Funnel numbers: Round 1 = 300 backbones → 282 AF2-tested → **1 pass** (pae 8.38); r1b = 1,000 backbones → 7,472 MPNN seqs → 5,275 ESMFold pass → 914 tested → **4 pass <10 (0.4%)**; Round 2 (partial diffusion, flat partial_T=18, 1,000 backbones) → **0% AF2 pass — shown to be an artifact of spec-score-biased backbone selection**; Round 3 (graduated partial_T 12–25) → 13 new AF2-passing; final 20 AF2-passing → pMHC-fold ΔPAE: **12 negative Δ + 1 deliberate non-specific control**; AF3: **1 strict pass** = `r3_r1b_870_87_dldesign_6` (AF2 pae **4.29**; AF3 on iptm **0.93**, binder–pep iptm **0.77**; AF3 ipSAE 0.510; pMHC-fold Δ −1.22; real CMS p5 18.6 Å² (outward-Asp 39.3); **Arg47:NH2↔Asp OD2 salt bridge 2.09–2.51 Å in 4/5 AF3 models**, Tyr78:OH↔OD1 3.4–3.7 Å in 2).
- Validated-design reference stats (from Liu data S1, computed by this repo): binders 59–106 aa; net charge −3 to −11 (median −7); 0 Cys; ΔSASA 2100–3300 Å² (median ~2900); rosetta ddG −57 to −88 REU (median −71); SC 0.51–0.71; packstat 0.56–0.71; **real CMS at p5 10.7–89.7 Å² (median 47.9)**; Dunbrack ipSAE (pae=10, dist=10) 0.42–0.87 (median 0.72); AF3 iptm 0.92–0.97; binder–pep iptm 0.77–0.91.
- Lessons: AF3 model_0 systematically predicts gauche⁻ Asp (OD buried) for every candidate near p5 — "always examine all 5 models"; a real `crop_size` bug in the cloned `pmhc_fold.py` silently cropped >256-residue queries (patched); the repo's own tier1 pipeline (explicit p5-polar-contact filter from the start) matched validated metrics (CMS p5 median 44.4 Å², ipSAE 0.92, 1 salt bridge at p5 median), whereas the later population lagged (CMS p5 median 5.9–10.5 Å²) — "enforce p5 polar contacts as a selection criterion from the start rather than discovered retrospectively".

## 6. 2026 papers/preprints citing or extending the Science papers; Motmaen 2023 network

- **Odin-Multi** (Sept 2026) — the direct 2026 extension of Liu/Johansen specificity screening: counter-selection as a design objective; lead S2 beats NY1-B04 on both avidity and M4A discrimination (see §3). https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.08.749745v1
- **Overath et al., "Predicting Experimental Success in De Novo Binder Design: A Meta-Analysis of 3,766 Experimentally Characterised Binders"** (bioRxiv 2025.08.14.670059; v2 2025-09-17; Jenkins group incl. Johansen-paper authors) — 15 targets, 200+ structural/energetic/confidence features per design; "interface-focused metrics, most notably the AF3-derived ipSAE outperform commonly used [metrics]"; proposes AF3 `ipSAE_min` ranking + structural filters. https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.08.14.670059v2
- **Householder et al., "De novo design and structure of a peptide-centric TCR mimic binding module"** (Science 389:375-379, same issue; preprint bioRxiv 2024.12.16.628822) — de novo α-helical TCRm for NY-ESO-1/HLA-A*02; **K_D = 9.5 nM**; **2.05-Å crystal structure**; "rigid TCR-like docking mode with an unusual degree of focus on the up-facing NY-ESO-1 side chains"; structure-informed in silico screen of **14,363 HLA-A*02 peptides** correctly predicted two off-target peptides while the TCRm maintained selectivity as a T-cell engager. https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.adv3813 · https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11702606/

**Motmaen 2023 specificity network (how to run it; availability)**
- Paper: Motmaen, Dauparas, Baek, Abedi, Baker, Bradley, PNAS 120(9):e2216697120 (2023), "Peptide-binding specificity prediction using fine-tuned protein structure prediction networks". https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2216697120 · https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9992841/
- Mechanism: a **classifier head on top of AlphaFold**; joint fine-tuning for classification + structure accuracy on **pMHC class I and II structural and binding data**; trained conservatively "after two epochs of training... to avoid excessive drift"; the fine-tuned model "developed a tendency to exclude non-binder peptides completely out of the MHC's peptide-binding groove"; approaches NetMHCpan accuracy on pMHC and generalises to SH3/PDZ.
- How to run: **GitHub https://github.com/phbradley/alphafold_finetune** (Apache-2.0; 176 stars; created 2022-07; ~32 commits) — includes `run_finetuning.py` with command-line parameters, a tiny pMHC example (`examples/tiny_pmhc_finetune/`), and a Colab pipeline (`alphafold_ft_colab_pipeline_v1.ipynb`). Example fine-tune command uses `--binder_intercepts 0.80367635 --binder_intercepts 0.43373787 --freeze_binder`. Requires AlphaFold2 params (`params_model_2_ptm.npz`).
- Availability caveat: README states the full peptide-MHC fine-tuning dataset "Zenodo upload is not working yet... dropbox link to the preliminary dataset" — i.e. **code is public; the full fine-tuned MHC-specific parameter set was not cleanly released in the original repo**. In practice the field runs it via derivatives: Liu et al. modified the AF2-fine-tuned-for-MHC into the "AF-MHC screening" module (repo https://github.com/77forest/pMHCI_binder_design, Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.15169815; pMHC_fold module runs `python ./batch_pMHC_fold.py --script ./pmhc_fold.py --alleles A*02:01 A*02:01 --peptides ... --minib_dir ...`), and ADAPT distributes its own fine-tuned AF2 (+RFantibody) parameters via Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.17488258. Also note the pMHCI repo's **2026 update: "We now recommend to use AF3 for folding pMHCs."** (https://github.com/77forest/pMHCI_binder_design/blob/main/README.md)
