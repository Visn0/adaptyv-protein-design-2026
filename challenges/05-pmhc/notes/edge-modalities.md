# Edge modalities against pMHC — non-minibinder approaches, KRAS G12D focus

**Date:** 2026-10-30. **Scope:** Challenge 5-relevant edges: non-IgG scaffolds, small molecules/macrocycles, de novo antibody/TCR design (2025-26), clinical/industrial KRAS G12D pMHC programs, and the patent space (novelty gate). Known basics (JDI TCR/7OW6, C8K10D TCRm, tebentafusp, ESK1 failure) are deliberately not repeated.
**Method:** ~70 web searches plus page, patent, and trial-registry extracts (ClinicalTrials.gov API v2, Google Patents, PubMed/PMC, company sites, SEC filings) on 2026-10-30. Every finding carries a URL. Items not confirmable from a primary source are tagged **[unverified]**.

## 1. Non-IgG modalities against pMHC

### 1.1 VHH / nanobody TCRms — the most productive non-IgG modality
- **HPV16 E6/HLA-A\*02:01 camel VHH (clone F5), CAR-T format.** Phage panning of camel VHH libraries on E6(29-38)/HLA-A\*02:01 monomers gave peptide-specific binders; F5 CAR-T killed HPV16+ cervical lines (CaSki, SS4050) in vitro and inhibited xenograft growth in mice; no binding to HLA-A\*24:02/A\*11:01 or unloaded HLA. Paper notes TCRm antibodies already exist for TP53 and KRAS neoantigens and that WT1- and AFP-TCRm therapeutics are in clinical trials. URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11546159
- **NIH patent on sdAbs vs HPV E6/E7–MHC** (five camel VHH sequences; F5 described): WO2024050399A1 / US20260070960A1 — "single-domain monoclonal antibodies that specifically bind HPV E6 or E7 oncogenic peptides in complex with MHC". URL: https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2024050399A1/en
- **VHH–VHH bispecific T-cell engager ("TCRm Bi-NbTE", Signal Transduct Target Ther 2026).** Modular anti-CD3ε VHH + TCRm VHH; two pMHC arms validated: HLA-A2/GPC3(144-152) and HLA-A2/WT1(126-134); KD ≈ 1.0–1.2 × 10⁻⁸ M (CD3ε) and ≈ 1 × 10⁻⁷ M (pMHC); no binding to mimetic peptides; specific killing of HLA-A2+/antigen+ tumor cells and primary HCC cells at E:T 10:1. URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13294373
- **Nanobody CARs vs HLA-A2/GPC3 and HLA-A2/WT1** (first reported pMHC-specific nanobodies for these targets, from human nanobody phage libraries): PMID 36411612. URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36411612/
- **Lessons:** VHHs (~15 kDa, ~600–900 Å² footprint) can achieve peptide-centric recognition; camelid/immune libraries panned on properly folded pMHC monomers work; formats are trivially modular (CAR, TCE, bispecific); but every campaign had to run allele- and peptide-scan counterscreens, and affinities land in the 10⁻⁷–10⁻⁹ M range — modest vs engineered TCRs.

### 1.2 DARPins — the one non-IgG scaffold with published pMHC structures
- **DARPin T-cell engagers for HLA/peptide (iScience 2025;28(12):113926; PMID 41321628; Venetz-Arenas et al.).** Bispecific DARPins simultaneously targeting HLA-I/peptide and CD3ε; strong peptide specificity by X-scanning mutagenesis and killing assays; **cryo-EM of the ternary DARPin/HLA-A\*02:01/NY-ESO-1(157-165) complex shows a rigid, concave DARPin interface**; no killing of NY-ESO-1-negative lines. URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12664392
- **Molecular Partners DARPin pMHC program.** AACR 2020 preclinical disclosure of "Peptide-MHC Programs" alongside MP0317/AMG 506: URL: https://investors.molecularpartners.com/node/6726/pdf . Jan-2025 investor deck states they "successfully selected specific DARPin binders against multiple pMHC targets … several self or non-self peptides restricted by different HLA class I alleles": URL: https://investors.molecularpartners.com/static-files/6b27ab9a-5742-44df-a1cf-df438ca24e80
- **PDB 9EPA "Crystal structure of DARPin NY_1"** (the NY-ESO-1 pMHC DARPin): URL: https://www.rcsb.org/structure/9EPA
- **Lesson:** repeat-protein scaffolds with rigid, concave paratopes read out peptide identity well; DARPin pMHC programs have existed for ~5 years at a large DARPin company yet no pMHC DARPin has entered the clinic — suggests specificity (not affinity) is the bottleneck.

### 1.3 Affibodies, Adnectins/fibronectins, Alphabodies, OBodies — no published pMHC binders found
- **Affibody:** repeated searches ("affibody pMHC", "affibody HLA-A2 peptide", "affibody KRAS") returned no published pMHC-binding affibody. The scaffold's clinical work is non-pMHC (e.g., FcRn half-life engineering: https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1417717111 ). **No pMHC data found.**
- **Adnectin / ¹⁰Fn3 (fibronectin) domains:** no pMHC binder found; Adnectin literature is about other targets (expanded binding footprint vs Ig: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22325775 ). The only clinical Adnectin (taldefgrobep alfa, myostatin) is non-pMHC: https://precision.fda.gov/ginas/app/ui/substances/18a4ffb3-2914-44c8-96e1-7e9c286434dc . **No pMHC data found.**
- **Alphabody (Complix):** pipeline is cell-penetrating alphabodies (intracellular targets) and earlier MSD oncology collaboration — no pMHC program disclosed: https://www.complix.com/pipeline , https://www.complix.com/our-technology/cell-penetrating-alphabodies . **No pMHC data found.**
- **OBody (OB-fold scaffold, OBodies Ltd, New Zealand):** scaffold characterized structurally (OBody NL8–lysozyme; PDB 4GLA; https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0086050 , https://www.rcsb.org/structure/4GLA ). **No pMHC data found.**
- **Lesson:** outside VHHs and DARPins, the pMHC-targeting scaffold space is essentially empty in the published record — a genuine white space (or a graveyard).

### 1.4 TCRm antibodies (IgG/scFv class) — the incumbent "alternative modality", with hard-won lessons
- First TCR-like antibody fragment: human scFv vs HLA-A1/MAGE-A1 (PNAS 2000): https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.97.14.7969
- TCRm antibody vs peptide/HLA class I in vivo (breast cancer models, 2010): https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20065111
- Pr20, therapeutic TCRm IgG1 vs PRAME/HLA-I (JCI 2017): https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28628042
- TCRm review (2021): https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8419142
- STAR-T functional screening of TCR-like antibodies against HLA-presented neoantigens (2025/26): https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13365543
- **Hardest lesson — potency/selectivity trade-off:** "Chimeric Antigen Receptors Directed at Mutant KRAS Exhibit an Inverse Relationship Between Functional Potency and Neoantigen Selectivity" (Cancer Res Commun 2022; A2 Biotherapeutics authors): https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9973398 . In practice, raising scFv affinity raised cross-reactivity with WT-peptide/related pMHC.
- **Format lesson:** mKRAS NeoCAR study (Cancer Cell 2025; scFvs vs KRAS G12V/HLA-A\*11:01) had to add inducible IL-12 **and** TCR knockout to widen the therapeutic window — binder affinity alone was insufficient for safety/efficacy in vivo: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12263316

## 2. Small molecules and macrocycles against pMHC — honest status: essentially nothing

**Bottom line: no published small molecule or macrocyclic peptide is known that discriminates a mutant peptide–MHC complex from its wild-type counterpart (or from peptide-free MHC).** Searches run ("small molecule peptide-MHC inhibitor", "small molecule discriminates mutant peptide MHC neoantigen", "macrocyclic peptide HLA/peptide binder", "molecular glue MHC class I", "DNA-encoded library pMHC", "peptidomimetic pMHC", "small molecule TCR mimic") returned no such precedent. What does exist is adjacent and instructive:

- **Drug-hapten pMHC neoepitopes (HapImmune, NYU → Aethon Therapeutics):** covalent KRAS G12C inhibitors (sotorasib, adagrasib) form a drug–peptide conjugate in the HLA groove; engineered antibodies (not small molecules) bind the *drug-modified* peptide/MHC with exquisite specificity. Cancer Discovery 2023: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9827112 ; PNAS 2024 (structures of antibodies vs multiple drug–peptide/MHC complexes; PDB 8VR9/8VRA): https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38781214 ; Nat Commun Dec 2025 therapeutic utility in vivo: https://aethontx.com/new-publication-in-nature-communications-demonstrates-the-therapeutic-utility-of-targeting-synthetic-neoantigens-generated-by-krasg12c-inhibitors ; company page: https://aethontx.com/hapimmune-platform ; patent (claims include VVVGACGVGK-KRAS G12C 10-mer conjugated to a covalent inhibitor presented by HLA-A\*11:01): https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2023250391A2/en
- **Small molecules CAN engage the HLA groove in sequence- and allele-specific ways — but as blockers, not mutant discriminators:** abacavir bound to HLA-B\*57:01+peptide (PDB 3VRI): https://www.rcsb.org/structure/3VRI ; small-molecule inhibitor of antigen binding/presentation by HLA-DR2b (J Immunol 2013): https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3891844 ; small molecule blocking thyroglobulin peptide binding to HLA (autoimmune thyroiditis): https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0021925820437135 ; peptidomimetic inhibitors of class II MHC antigen presentation (1999; PDB 3O3A/3O3B with HLA-A2): https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10385320
- **Macrocycles vs MHC protein–protein interfaces exist (not pMHC discrimination):** macrocyclic peptides inhibiting the LAG-3/MHC class II interaction (WO2025193572A1, WO2025193574A1): https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2025193770A1/en ; RaPID macrocyclic-peptide platform (no published pMHC selection found): https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34505781/
- **Lesson for the competition:** small-molecule discrimination of a single-residue peptide difference on a class I pMHC has no published precedent — treat it as an open problem; the only proven "small molecule × pMHC" synergy is the drug-hapten strategy, which requires a covalent drug target (G12C), not G12D.

## 3. De novo antibody / TCR design against pMHC (2024–2026)

### 3.1 The Baker/Garcia-axis papers (the core 2025 results)
- **Liu et al., Science 2025, "Design of high-specificity binders for peptide–MHC-I complexes" (Science 389:386-391; PMID 40705892).** RFdiffusion + ProteinMPNN mini-proteins that "arc above the peptide binding groove … and make extensive contacts with the peptide"; **specific binders identified for ten target pMHCs** that bind on-target tetramer but not closely related peptides; authors note learning which "scaffold geometries give the most effective readouts of sequence over the full peptide length." URLs: https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.adv0185 , preprint: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11623666 , code/data: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15169815
- **Householder et al., Science 2025, "De novo design and structure of a peptide-centric TCR mimic binding module" (Science 389:375-379; PMID 40705894).** A designed 4-helix-bundle mini-TCRm vs NY-ESO-1/HLA-A\*02: 2.05 Å crystal structure (PDB 9MIN) shows a **rigid, diagonal TCR-like docking footprint at ~40° to the pMHC groove, burying 1216 Å² with 31% of the interface from the peptide**; two shape-complementary pockets capture the Met4-Trp5 bulge independently (vs one big CDR pocket in Fab/TCR); structure-informed in-silico screen predicted low off-target reactivity. URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12313176 (Science: https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.adv3813 )
- **"De novo-designed pMHC binders facilitate T cell–mediated cytotoxicity toward cancer cells" (Science 2025, 389(6758); PMID 40705893; Johansen, Wolff, … Jenkins, Hadrup).** Rapid de novo minibinder pipeline; **in-silico cross-panning for specificity prescreening**; MD simulations improved in-vitro success prediction; high-affinity NY-ESO-1 binder plus a binder to a neoantigen pMHC of unknown structure (RVTDESILSY/HLA-A\*01:01); binders work as CAR recognition domains. URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40705893
- **Motmaen et al., bioRxiv 2025-11-19, "Targeting peptide–MHC complexes with designed T cell receptors and antibodies" (PMID 41332722; Garcia + Baker + Bradley labs).** Introduces **ADAPT (Antigen-receptor Design Against Peptide-MHC Targets)**, a structure-based deep-learning framework that designs **both TCRs and antibodies**; designs scored with AF2/RF2 pMHC–TCR PAE terms plus CDR3 RMSD; **cryo-EM of two designed antibodies bound to their pMHC targets shows atomic-level accuracy at the recognition interface**; designed TCRs activate T cells; runs on commodity GPUs (11 GB VRAM). URLs: https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.11.19.689381v1 , https://europepmc.org/article/PPR/PPR1122741 . *The full target list (and whether KRAS G12D/A\*11:01 is included) was not retrievable from the abstract text — [unverified].*
- **Geometry lessons from these:** designed binders adopt **diagonal (~40°) TCR-like docking**; peptide-centric contacts dominate (30-40% of buried surface from peptide for the mini-TCRm); scaffolds "arc over" the groove; success correlates with readout of the full peptide length. Antibody-format designs in ADAPT use both chains (Fab-like scFv), whereas RFantibody/Nabla VHH designs are single-domain.

### 3.2 RFantibody and other general antibody-design engines
- **Bennett et al., Nature 2026, "Atomically accurate de novo design of antibodies with RFdiffusion" (Nature 649:183-193; PMID 41193805; epublished 2025-11-05).** Fine-tuned RFdiffusion² designs VHHs, scFvs and full antibodies against user-specified epitopes with atomic precision; best scFv Kd 72 nM, converted full IgG1 68 nM. **No pMHC target reported in the retrieved abstract [unverified].** URLs: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09721-5 , https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12727541 , code: https://github.com/RosettaCommons/RFantibody ; commercialized as a VHH design service by Ranomics: https://www.ranomics.com/technology/rfantibody
- **Nabla Bio "JAM-2 pMHC multispecifics" (June 2026 technical report).** JAM-2 designed **VHH binders directly from the pMHC amino-acid sequence**, pooled library screened on pMHC-I tetramers; binders across five targets; **cryo-EM of a VHH/KRAS G12V:pMHC complex matched the prediction to 0.93 Å average backbone deviation**; multispecific T-cell engagers directed human T cells against KRAS-mutant cells in vitro (no animal data). URLs: https://nabla-public.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/2026_Nabla_JAM2_multispecific_pMHC.pdf , news summary: https://eternalsearch.net/news/260626004 , platform: https://www.nabla.bio/platform
- **Chai Discovery (Chai-2 / Chai-2-mab, June 2026).** Company-reported full-mAb de novo design; a GEN article figure shows a **Chai-designed antibody bound to KRAS G12V pMHC with negligible binding to WT-KRAS and KRAS G12D complexes (single-residue discrimination)** [company/PR-level, no peer-reviewed paper found — unverified]. URLs: https://www.genengnews.com/topics/cancer/novel-antibody-technologies-strive-for-clinical-impact/ , https://www.chaidiscovery.com/news/chai-2-mab
- **In-silico→in-vitro TCRm discovery vs KRAS G12D₁₀/HLA-C\*08:02 (2026; PMID 42237541).** Integrated computational design + limited CDR diversification + yeast display yielded high-affinity, selective antibodies to KRAS G12D₁₀/HLA-C\*08:02; CAR and bispecific T-cell engager formats gave selective cytotoxicity. URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42237541
- **TCRm antibodies to mutated KRAS by HLA-A\*02:01-restricted CAR/TCE (2025; PMID 40794198):** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40794198 ; PMC: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12449438
- **Biocytogen RenTCR-mimic mouse platform (immunization-based TCRm):** fully human antibodies to G12V/HLA and G12D/HLA complexes; higher affinity than endogenous TCRs; potent in-vitro tumor lysis as CD3 TCEs; good off-target profile (AACR 2024 #6717): https://aacrjournals.org/cancerres/article/84/6_Supplement/6717/741222/Abstract-6717-Targeting-mutant-KRAS-proteins-with ; earlier G12V/HLA abstract (JITC 2023 #1168): https://jitc.bmj.com/content/jitc/11/Suppl_1/A1287.full.pdf ; TCRm asset pipeline (WT1, NY-ESO-1, KRAS G12V/HLA-A03, KRAS G12V/HLA-A11, gp100, MAGE-A4): https://biocytogen.com/drug-development/tcr-mimic-assets

## Gaps / open questions

1. **No small molecule or macrocyclic peptide that discriminates mutant vs WT pMHC exists in the published record** (searches listed in §2). Treat as white space; the only validated "small-molecule × pMHC" path is covalent-drug hapten + antibody.
2. **Affibody, Adnectin/¹⁰Fn3, Alphabody, OBody: zero published pMHC data found** — absence of evidence (searches enumerated in §1.3), not proof of absence of internal/patent work.
3. **ADAPT (Motmaen 2025) target list not retrievable** from abstract-level text; whether it includes KRAS G12D/A\*11:01 is **[unverified]**. Same for RFantibody (no pMHC target reported) and Chai-2-mab (PR-level only).
4. **Nabla JAM-2 pMHC report is a company technical report** (no peer review; no in-vivo data); the five targets are not all identifiable from the retrieved text (KRAS G12V confirmed) — **[unverified]** for G12D coverage.
5. **IMC-KRASG12D appears dormant/undisclosed in late 2026** (no trial registration, absent from pipeline page and Q2-2026 release; Synapse says preclinical) — status **[unverified]** but consistently negative across sources.
6. **Patent assignees needing face-check:** WO2021097365A2 (Gritstone Bio per Patentscope search hit — confirm on patent face), EP4656655/US20260240907A1/US20260297159A1 (Chinese TCR filers), US20260027208A1, NDV Therapeutics company identity.
7. **Corrections to common assumptions:** AFNT-211 is **KRAS G12V** (AFNT-212 is the G12D product); KT18 is **G12V(9-mer)**/A\*11:01; KDA11 is G12D(7-16)/A\*11:01 academic-stage; the C8K10D TCR space is NCI-patented (Tran/Rosenberg family).
