# Equilibrium-ensemble test of the L58K reading (BioEmu2, 2026-11-02)

## Method

- System: the `repair-wave2-pep__run-016__design_0005__g12d-L58K` complex
  (chains A HLA 276, B b2m 99, C peptide 10, D binder 72).
- `bioemu2 convert-boltz` from the design's Boltz-2 YAML, then 31 contacts
  added from the folded pose (peptide x binder CA-CA <= 9 A pairs plus the
  D6-R114 and D6-Q70 anchors, `max_distance 10.0`). Validated with
  `load_system_and_contacts`.
- `bioemu2 sample-equilibrium --num-samples 16` for both peptide states
  (G12D: `conditioned-g12d.yaml`; WT: `conditioned-wt.yaml`).
- First attempt without contacts dissociated (binder 28 A from the peptide in
  every frame) - the converter drops Boltz contact fields; conditioning is
  mandatory for complexes.

## Result - G12D state (16/16 frames pass physicality)

| read | value |
|---|---|
| K58 NZ to D6 carboxylate | mean 7.4 A, sd 0.8, range 5.8-9.7 |
| D6 to R114 anchor (sanity) | mean 3.2 A, sd 0.8 (holds) |
| closest binder atom to P6 CA | mean 14 A (interface breathes; min 3.8) |
| binder CA RMSD vs frame 0 | mean 1.5 A (stays folded) |

## Verdict

The L58K reading is persistent under thermal motion: K58 never leaves
5.8-9.7 A of the mutant carboxylate across the ensemble. The mean 7.4 A is
the same geometry class as the JDI's published indirect reading (7.51 A) -
field reading, not a direct salt bridge. The binder's peptide interface
breathes, but the charge-reading contact does not disengage. The mechanism
is not a static-pose artifact.

WT control: sampled with the same contacts from the WT folded pose. Same-metric
contrast (K58 NZ to P6 CA): G12D 5.0 +- 0.7 A, WT 4.5 +- 0.4 A. K58 NZ to its
closest target atom: 2.9 A in BOTH states (sd 0.6 vs 0.3) - a permanent
structural H-bond/anchor, not the reading.

## Honest limit

The ensembles demonstrate persistence, not state discrimination. The K58's
placement is backbone-anchored and nearly identical in both states; the
difference between states is the ABSENCE of the carboxylate in the WT, which a
shape-based equilibrium sampler (no explicit charges) cannot score. This is
consistent with the project's central lesson: only the E-score (explicit
electrostatics, control-validated) reads specificity; ensemble samplers read
stability and geometry. Use: ensemble = stability/persistence evidence, never
a specificity instrument.
