# Deep dive: physical and chemical lessons from TCR-mimic antibodies and engineered TCRs against peptide-MHC

For Challenge 5 (de novo binder against KRAS G12D peptide VVVGADGVGK / HLA-A*11:01 + b2m; must NOT bind WT VVVGAGGVGK or peptide-free MHC). Read-only literature pass, 2026-10-30. Every finding carries a URL; numbers are quoted from the sources. Items I could not verify are marked UNVERIFIED.

Epitope orientation for reference: in the KRAS 7-16 decamer VVVGADGVGK the G12D substitution is peptide position 6 (D6); WT differs only there. In the unbound pMHC, residues 4-6 form the central bulge (p6 exposed, pointing up); upon TCR engagement the peptide undergoes an induced fit and p6 flips DOWN into the HLA F-pocket (see Section 0). This is the single most important mechanistic fact found for this exact epitope.

## 0. The closest published analogue: the JDI TCR / IMC-KRASG12D bispecific (same peptide, same HLA)

Poole et al., Nat Commun 2022, Immunocore. A human TCR (JDI; TRAV19*01 / TRBV6-2*01) was isolated from an HLA-A*11+ healthy donor and engineered into a soluble TCR x anti-CD3 bispecific.
- Parental JDI TCR: KD = 63 uM for HLA-A*11:01-KRAS G12D (VVVGADGVGK); NO measurable binding to KRAS WT (VVVGAGGVGK), G12C, ERAS, RRAS, MRAS, DIRAS2, RAP1A, or 28 HLA-A*11-presented self-peptides (pools). URL: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-022-32811-1
- Alanine scan of the peptide: D6, G4, A5 and G9 are the most critical for TCR binding (D6 = the mutation). Same URL.
- Affinity maturation (NNK CDR libraries, with depletion against WT pHLA before positive selection): 3 generations took KD from 63 uM to 261 nM (window 98x), 743 pM (window >4000x), and 64 pM (window >6000x); total ~1,000,000-fold affinity increase WITH retained (indeed widened) discrimination of G12D vs WT; t1/2 >= 20 h. Same URL.
- Structures (PDB 7OW3/7OW4 = pHLA alone; 7OW5/7OW6 = high-affinity TCR complexes; 7PB2 = parent TCR complex): the TCR binds both WT and G12D pMHC in a virtually identical mode (TCR V-domain RMSD 0.29 A C-alpha; crossing angle 50 degrees; all CDRs except CDR2b contact; CDR3b engages mainly the peptide C-terminus). Polar contacts to the peptide BACKBONE: Y33a to A5, N101b and H100b to G7, G98b to G9. Same URL.
- Discrimination mechanism: D6 is buried in the HLA groove in the TCR-bound state, forming a salt bridge to HLA R114 (stabilized by D116) plus an H-bond to Q70 - impossible for WT G6. Binding is enthalpically driven, with a much larger favorable enthalpy for G12D (dH = -20.3 vs -9.6 kcal/mol; TdS = -8.1 vs -1.8 kcal/mol); MMPBSA difference 12.6 kcal/mol vs experimental 10.8. Specificity comes from indirect electrostatics (G12D makes the HLA surface more negative; TCR residues R50a, K70a, K94b, W53a benefit). Same URL.
- Specificity profile of the 64 pM TCR (JDIa96b35): panned against an HLA-A*11 pHLA phage library of ~6.6 x 10^9 variants; 452 unique peptides; the top 5 (96 percent of reads) share a GADG motif at positions 4-7; of 20 motif-derived self-peptides, 18 showed no binding; RASL10A peptide bound at 460 nM, DNHD1 at 2.3 uM (weak). As an ImmTAC (IMC-KRASG12D), EC50 = 4.6 pM on peptide-pulsed cells and 28.5 pM on CL40 colon cancer cells; no response to KRAS WT cell lines or normal cardiac, pulmonary and colon cells. Same URL.

## 1. ESK1 (WT1/HLA-A*02:01) and the NY-ESO-1 TCRm antibodies (3M4E5/3M4F4)

ESK1, Dao et al., Sci Transl Med 2013 (MSKCC; fully human IgG1 from a phage-display scFv library):
- Target: WT1 peptide RMFPNAPYL (WT1 126-134) / HLA-A*0201. ESK1 bound leukemia and solid tumor lines and primary leukemias in a WT1- and HLA-A0201-dependent manner with KD = 0.1 nM (radioimmunoassay on cells; 'sub-nanomolar, over 1000-fold tighter than natural TCRs'). Activity was Fc-dependent (F(ab')2 inactive; ADCC mechanism); a glyco-variant (ESKM) had 5-10x better ADCC. URL: https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/scitranslmed.3005661
- Specificity controls: no binding to T2 cells alone, or pulsed with RHAMM-R3, an Ewing sarcoma peptide, or the heteroclitic WT1-A1 peptide (all HLA-A2 binders); no binding to HLA-A2+ cells in HLA-A2 transgenic mouse lymphoid organs. Peptide position-1 substitutions (WT1-A1/B) reduced ESK1 binding most. Same URL.
- Comparators cited in the same paper: other peptide TCRm mAbs had affinities of NY-ESO-1 47 nM, gp100 294 nM, PR1 9.9 nM. Same URL.

ESK1 structure, Ataie et al., J Mol Biol 2016 (PDB 4WUU):
- ESK1 engages HLA-A*02:01 mainly through cation-pi networks: CDR-H2 and CDR-H3 (four tyrosines at H2-55, H3-101, H3-104, H3-105) stack against HLA Arg108, Arg169 and Arg170; peptide contacts concentrate at the peptide N-terminus (Arg1, Phe3, Pro4). Peptide position-4 Cys substitution loses 10-fold (steric clash with Asp94); bulky residues at position 5 lose >10-fold. URL: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmb.2015.12.002
- HLA-subtype promiscuity: high-affinity binding is driven by contacts to regions shared between at least six common HLA-A*02 subtypes; ESK1 binds the same WT1 peptide on A*02:01, 02:02, 02:03, 02:05, 02:06, 02:07 and 02:11 (tested). This expands the eligible population but also the off-target surface. Same URL.
- Structure-guided off-target prediction: BLAST for N-terminal RMF-homologous peptides plus NetMHC < 500 nM identified MED13L (RMFPTPPSL) and PIGQ (RMFPGEVAL); ESK1 bound cells pulsed with BOTH peptides - i.e. the predicted off-targets were real. ESK1 also bound a subset of healthy-donor CD19+ B cells (WT1-negative). Rule stated: peptides homologous at the N-terminus that keep the position-9 HLA-A*02 anchor can be off-targets even if positions 5-8 differ. Same URL.

ESK1 off-target interactome, Nat Commun 2024 (the 'why ESK1 never became a drug' paper):
- ESK1 'has never entered clinical development due to off-target binding and lysis of diverse human cells'. MS-based immune-enrichment from primary human LIVER tissue recovered 16 HLA-A*02:01 peptides bound by ESK1 (motif: R/K/A at P1, A2 anchors maintained), confirmed off-target-induced T cell activation and ESK1-mediated killing of liver spheroids. Example peptide: ILF2 127-135 (KILPTLEAV). The mimic motif engages two separate positions and was missed by earlier sequence-based predictions; the two shortlists did not overlap. URL: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-47062-5

NY-ESO-1 TCRm antibodies, Stewart-Jones et al., PNAS 2009 (3M4E5 and 3M4F4 Fabs; PDB 3HAE):
- Structures of two Fabs bound to the NY-ESO-1 157-165 (C9V) analogue SLLMWITQV / HLA-A*0201 show TCR-like binding: the Fabs and the 1G4 TCR bind the central Met-Trp peptide motif in almost identical orientation and share HLA contact residues (R65, K66, A69, Q72, T73, A150, Q155), including hydrogen bonds to the same atoms (R65 NE, T73 OG, A150 O, Q155 E1). The Met-Trp 'peg' dominates the Fab-peptide contacts. URL: https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.0901425106
- Affinity maturation: a peptide-focused second-generation library yielded a Fab with 2 light-chain mutations, 20-fold affinity improvement to 2-4 nM - 1000-fold above the TCR; grafted as a recombinant TCR it conferred specific killing of NY-ESO-1/HLA-A2 target cells. Same URL.
- Cross-reactivity of 3M4E5 itself: no dedicated off-target dataset found in this pass (UNVERIFIED). Later work: AlphaFold3 models TCRm Fv-pMHC complexes for 34 NY-ESO-1/A2 TCRm CAR clones and for 3M4E5/3M4F4 (ipTM 0.93); VL domains contact the alpha1 helix plus the peptide C-terminus, VH contacts alpha2 plus the peptide middle - a balanced, TCR-like geometry, and the selected clones showed no cross-reactivity. URL: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/19420862.2026.2719263

## 2. Tebentafusp (gp100/HLA-A*02:01 ImmTAC)

- Format and target: affinity-enhanced soluble TCR (disulfide-stabilized alpha/beta) fused to an anti-CD3 scFv (~77 kDa); binds gp100 280-288 peptide YLEPGPVTA on HLA-A*02:01 'with picomolar affinity'. URL: https://aacrjournals.org/clincancerres/article/26/22/5869/82887/ ; product/mechanism review: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/21645515.2026.2675068
- Affinity engineering path: native TCR-pHLA affinities are 10-300 uM; ImmTAC TCRs were affinity-matured ~10^6-fold into the picomolar range (phage display on disulfide-stabilized TCRs), increasing target half-life from seconds to hours; the anti-CD3 arm is nanomolar, ~1000x weaker than the TCR arm so that pHLA recognition drives activity. Platform paper: https://www.nature.com/articles/nm.2764 ; engineering: https://www.nature.com/articles/nbt1070 ; review with the numbers: https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6694/11/7/971 ; example potency: tebentafusp at 80 pM kills gp100+ HLA-A*02:01+ uveal melanoma cells in vitro. Same MDPI URL.
- What the TCR domain touches: no public crystal/cryo-EM structure of the tebentafusp TCR domain in complex with gp100/HLA-A*02:01 was found in this pass (UNVERIFIED; searched Europe PMC and PDB). The closest structural work on gp100-directed TCRs is on other gp100 TCRs (molecular-switch study): https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M115.707414
- Documented cross-reactivity screening (the ImmTAC preclinical safety package): (i) human cell testing across a wide range of normal and cancer cells (no animal models; ~40 percent of the mouse MHC-I peptidome is human-identical, so mouse models are uninformative); (ii) molecular analysis of the TCR peptide-binding motif to find cross-reactive mimic peptides in the genome; (iii) alloreactivity assays across a panel covering all HLA types with frequency >10 percent; (iv) alanine scans of the target peptide (e.g. NY-ESO-1 SLLMWITQC variants for an NY-ESO-1 ImmTAC at 0.1 nM). Example result: ImmTAC-mageA3 caused no significant IFN-gamma release across 25 donors of diverse HLA type. URL: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0205491
- Clinical cross-reactivity profile: the dominant toxicities are on-target off-tumor (rash from melanocytes; CRS 89 percent, mostly grade 1-2) rather than off-target peptide cross-reactivity; regulatory review also included IHC on 35 normal tissues. URLs: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/21645515.2026.2675068 and https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2022/761228Orig1s000MultidisciplineR.pdf

## 3. p53 R175H/HLA-A*02:01 TCRm antibody H2

Hsiue et al., Science 2021 (PDB 6W51):
- Peptide HMTEVVRHC (p53 168-176; mutant His at position 8, Arg174 at position 7 both pointing UP out of the groove; non-canonical anchors Met at P2 and Cys at P9). MS: ~700 copies/cell when co-expressed in COS-7 cells. H2-scDb (bispecific single-chain diabody) bound p53R175H/HLA-A*02:01 with KD = 86 nM (SPR; kon 1.76 x 10^5 /Ms, koff 1.48 x 10^-2 /s), no detectable binding to WT p53/HLA-A*02:01. URL: https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.abc8697
- Where it touches (6W51, 3.5 A): docking angle 36 degrees, unusually PERPENDICULAR to the canonical diagonal TCR/TCRm mode. 79 contacts (<=4 A) to the HLA alpha1/alpha2 helices; the LIGHT chain contributes 61 percent of HLA contacts; buried SASA 818 A2 (427 light + 391 heavy). Only 4 of 6 CDRs touch the peptide (H1, H2, H3, L3); 36 peptide contacts including 5 H-bonds. His175 makes 47 percent of ALL direct contacts; the CDRs form a cage around the peptide C-terminus with the His175 imidazole anchored by Asp54 (CDR-H2) and Tyr94 (CDR-L3), and Tyr52 (CDR-H2) acting as a 'ceiling' capping the cage via pi-pi stacking. Same URL.
- Mechanism of discrimination: NOT indirect/desolvation-only - it is direct and steric. Lack of WT recognition is attributed to the WT arginine at position 8 colliding with the Tyr52 'ceiling' of the cage; positions 3-6 are also read via van der Waals contacts. (The task brief's 'no direct contacts with the mutation; desolvation energetics' does not match the Hsiue data: His175 is the dominant contact. The indirect/electrostatic mechanism applies instead to the KRAS G12D TCR in Section 0.) Same URL.
- Off-target screening: 171 single-substitution peptides (each position to all 19 other residues) tested on T2 cells; changes at P7 and P8 abolished recognition while keeping HLA binding. A 20-percent-reactivity motif was derived: x-[ILMVNQTC]-[ST]-[DE]-[IV]-[IMVST]-R-H-[AILVGHSTYC]; a human-proteome ScanProsite search found STAT2, VPS13A, ZFP3 - none bound (T2 pulsing and COS-7 expression), so no cross-reactive peptide was found. Same URL.
- Affinity engineering (DiNapoli et al., JCI 2026): original H2 scDb KD = 29.5 nM; variants F53S(L2) 12.9 nM, Y57I(H2) 6.8 nM, F53S+Y57I 3.3 nM; the 3.3 nM scDb had a 7x lower EC50 (4.2 vs 31 pM) and killed TYK-nu cells carrying only 1.5 copies of R175H/A2 per cell, with no off-target activity on isogenic TP53-knockout cells; better tumor control in mice, well tolerated. Strikingly, in CAR and STAR (TCR-embedded) formats, increasing affinity DECREASED function ('CAR T cells present a Goldilocks situation'); specificity was maintained in all formats. URL: https://www.jci.org/articles/view/192885
- Clinical-stage follow-on (CLSP-1025, Clin Cancer Res 2026): an IgG-like Fc-fusion TCE against p53R175H/HLA-A*02:01; KD(CD3) = 1.2 nM and KD(pMHC) = 12.2 nM; no measurable binding to WT p53/HLA-A*02:01 up to 400 nM; 172-peptide positional scan; cross-reactivity screen across 264 HLA class I alleles (>99 percent of 4-digit haplotypes); mutation-dependent killing of lines and patient organoids. URL: https://aacrjournals.org/clincancerres/article/doi/10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-26-1252
- TCR-side comparison: three p53R175H/HLA-A2 TCRs were crystallized (Wu et al., Nat Commun 2020) showing how the driver mutation rendered a self-peptide immunogenic; useful as a natural-readout benchmark. URL: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-020-16755-y

## 4. C8K10D: KRAS G12D/HLA-C*08:02 TCR-like antibody (Ahn et al., Mol Ther 2026; PDB 9WJ6)

- Target: KRAS G12D decamer GADGVGKSAL / HLA-C*08:02/b2m (the NT-112 clinical epitope; NT-112 is in phase 1/2, NCT06218914). URL: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ymthe.2026.05.032
- Discovery workflow: in silico docking of existing TCR-like antibody/pMHC complexes onto KRAS(G12D)10/HLA-C*08 (PDB 6ULK) + ProteinMPNN CDR design + yeast-display library randomizing 3-4 residues in CDR-H3/L3, selected with target tetramers and counter-selected with SARS peptide (ITDAVDCAL)/HLA-C*08. Parental C8K10D Fab: KD ~1.36 uM; no binding to SARS/HLA-C*08 or p53(R175H)/HLA-A*02. Same URL.
- Affinity-maturation path: CDR-H1/H2 library + monomeric selections -> C8K10D3/5/6 ~20 nM (C8K10D5 = 17.4 nM; ~70-fold over parent). Same URL.
- Peptide-free/irrelevant-pMHC failure and its fix (directly relevant to Challenge 5): the ~20 nM IgG clones bound KRAS-pulsed cells but ALSO bound SARS-pulsed and UNPULSED K562(HLA-C*08:02+/TAP-) cells above background. A CDR-L2 sub-library was then screened with negative selection on SARS-pulsed and unpulsed cells and positive selection on KRAS G12D-pulsed cells, yielding C8K10D5-1 (KD 5 nM) and C8K10D5-18 (27 nM) with no detectable binding to unpulsed or control-peptide cells. Same URL.
- Contacts (9WJ6, 4.5 A; peptide-centric): CDR-H3, CDR-L1 and CDR-L3 cover the entire peptide surface; HLA-C*08:02 contacts are minor. Asp3p (the G12D mutation) does NOT contact the Fab - it lies below the peptide plane making an ionic interaction with HLA Arg156. Lys7p makes ionic/H-bond contacts with Asp31L and Tyr29L (light chain), flanked by Met106H and Gln155(HLA). Same URL.
- Alanine-scan hotspots: no binding with substitutions at peptide positions 3, 7 or 10 (P3 = the mutation, P7 = Lys, P10 = the C-terminal anchor), and no binding to G12V, G12C or G12R peptides - despite P3 being an indirect (HLA-anchored) readout, P3 identity controls binding. Same URL.
- Comprehensive specificity: phage single-chain-trimer library randomizing P3-P9 (3.3 x 10^10 transformants); 156 enriched peptides; no human-proteome matches; 4/7 tested peptides bound, and all 4 binding peptides carried Asp at P3; CAR-T cells showed no lysis with WT KRAS10 (GAGGVGKSAL) or the dominant enriched peptide (GACAFTNTIL). Strict HLA-C*08:02 restriction (tested C*03:04, C*07:02, C*05:01, C*04:01 with/without peptide). Same URL.
- Structure-guided optimization: C8K10D5-18-DE and -DQE variants improved yield (244/340 mg/L from barely detectable) and affinity to 2.62/2.38 nM; selectivity retained; in silico immunogenicity comparable to the parental template. Same URL.
- Note the low resolution (4.5 A): side-chain contact statements are partly model-supported, and C8K10D5-18 interactions are inferred from the C8K10D5-1 structure. The authors also flag that AlphaFold2 predictions of the interface disagreed with the crystal structure (low pLDDT on peptide/CDRs). Same URL.

## 5. KRAS G12V TCRm-ADCs, 2026 AACR TCRm-ADCs, and computational TCRm design (2023-2026)

KRAS G12V TCRm-ADC (Shen et al., Asian J Pharm Sci 2020):
- Target: 10-mer KRAS peptide KLVVVGAVGV (G12V; mutation at peptide position 8) presented by HLA-A*0201; the TCRm 2G1 was engineered from Skora et al.'s anti-KRAS G12V/HLA-A2 scFv into human IgG1; cysteine variants 2E8 and 2A5 allowed site-specific MMAE conjugation (DAR 1.85 and 3.65). 2G1/2E8/2A5 bound G12V/HLA-A2 on T2 cells and did NOT bind G12D or WT peptide complexes; 2A5 had the highest affinity. URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7750800/
- Functional limits: SW480 (HLA-A2+/KRAS G12V+) was not killed because surface G12V/HLA-A2 is below FACS detection (limit ~300-500/cell); killing required peptide loading or a ubiquitin-KRAS G12V fusion cell line. IC50 112.8 nM (2A5-MMAE) vs 214.6 nM (2E8-MMAE) - about 700-fold weaker than conventional TAA-ADCs; 66-75 percent internalized in 2.5 h; 2A5-MMAE active in vivo at 20 mg/kg. Same URL.

AACR 2026 abstract 6929 (NDV-ADC01-exatecan; New Deantronics):
- A TCRm identified by phage display/ELISA/cell assays with 'high specificity and affinity' for KRAS G12D/HLA-A*11 pMHC; converted to an exatecan ADC; specific in vitro/in vivo antitumor activity in lung, pancreatic and colorectal xenografts without obvious toxicity; synergy with low-dose radiotherapy in a humanized HLA-A*11/hB2M transgenic model. Epitope geometry, affinity values and the WT/peptide-free counter-screens are NOT disclosed in the abstract (UNVERIFIED beyond the abstract). URL: https://aacrjournals.org/cancerres/article/86/7_Supplement/6929/780899/Abstract-6929-Targeting-intracellular-KRASG12D-HLA

ADAPT (Motmaen, Jude, Wang, ..., Baker, Garcia; bioRxiv 2025-11-19, 10.1101/2025.11.19.689381):
- ADAPT (Antigen-receptor Design Against Peptide-MHC Targets) is a structure-based deep-learning pipeline combining fine-tuned AlphaFold2 conformational sampling, ProteinMPNN sequence design and a genetic-algorithm refinement loop; it designs BOTH TCRs and TCR-mimic antibodies against chosen pMHC targets. URL: https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.11.19.689381v1
- Results: designed TCRs against six target pMHCs bound their intended targets; 'some off-target binding, particularly to the A02-TLM pMHC' was observed (explicit cross-reactivity caveat); AF3 metrics correlated with experimental binding. TCR-like antibodies were designed against three pMHCs with relatively high success in small-scale screens; the pipeline supports 'forward' vs 'reverse' docking geometry (heavy chain aligned with TCR beta or TCR alpha), controlled at the docking step. Cryo-EM structures of two designed antibodies bound to their pMHC targets (PDB 9YTF = vAB-30/A01-EVD; 9YTD = vAB-66/A02-PAP) show atomic-level accuracy. Specificity observation: mutations at non-contacted peptide positions were better tolerated than at positions with extensive contacts. Code: https://github.com/phbradley/ADAPT ; data: https://zenodo.org/records/17488258
- RFantibody (Bennett et al., Nature 2026, DOI 10.1038/s41586-025-09721-5) is the general antibody-design counterpart (fine-tuned RFdiffusion; VHHs to four disease epitopes, cryo-EM-validated); its targets are NOT pMHC - for pMHC the ADAPT work is the relevant computational result. URL: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09721-5

De novo alpha-helical TCR mimic against NY-ESO-1/HLA-A*02 (Householder et al., Science 2025; PDB 9MIN):
- Designed a 99-aa four-helix-bundle 'mini-TCRm' with RFdiffusion + ProteinMPNN over the up-facing Met4/Trp5/Thr7/Gln8 hotspots; 2 of 5 top designs bound NY-ESO-1 tetramer and not MART-1; SPR KD = 9.5 nM; 2.05 A structure shows a rigid, diagonal (40 degrees) TCR-like footprint, 1216 A2 buried (31 percent peptide-specific); the Met4-Trp5 bulge inserts into two hydrophobic pockets with 3 H-bonds to the peptide plus 5 H-bonds and 1 salt bridge to HLA-A*02. It contacts 19 MHC residues vs 14 for the 3M4E5 Fab and 13 for the 1G4 TCR - but buries the smallest PROPORTION of peptide surface, and still achieved the highest affinity and peptide specificity, i.e. focused peptide contacts plus dispersed MHC contacts. URL: https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.adv3813
- Off-target screening and windows: Hamming-distance search against 14,363 MS-detected HLA-A*02 9-mers found only 5 peptides at distance 4 (plus 2 with an exact Met4-Trp5 motif); a ProteinMPNN 'off-target score' predicted PIP5K1A (off-target 3) and KIRREL2 (off-target 5), both confirmed as weak binders by T2 staining. As a T cell engager: EC50 9.1 nM on NY-ESO-1, no activation by off-target 3, EC50 174 nM for off-target 5 (about a 19x potency window); between 1-10 nM the TCE retained target selectivity. Same URL.

Other computational/engineering items:
- WT1 VH single-domain antibody (Oh et al., Comput Struct Biotechnol J 2026; same group as C8K10D): computationally designed human VH that contacts all 9 peptide residues of RMF/HLA-A*02 and does not bind a control pMHC; 81 nM monovalent (HSA-DIII fusion), 0.37 nM apparent when bivalent; works as CAR and as a bivalent TCE. URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13669508/
- KDA11-01/02 (Oncol Res 2024): two TIL-derived TCRs against the SAME challenge peptide, KRAS G12D 7-16 (VVVGADGVGK) / HLA-A*11:01; functional avidity EC50 50 nM (KDA11-01) and 13 nM (KDA11-02); no response to G12C/G12V/nonamer/WT peptides; alloreactivity panel of 63 HLA cell lines (covering >85 percent of the Chinese population) showed negligible responses. Caveat flagged in the paper: KDA11-02 showed some cytotoxicity with the G12C peptide (KC2) without IFN-gamma - a possible cross-reactivity signal or an assay artefact. URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11576958/
- Sim et al., PNAS 2020 (HLA-C*08:02 G12D TCRs from the famous ACT case): only the G12D peptide (not WT) stabilizes HLA-C*08:02, because D3 forms a critical anchor salt bridge to HLA-C - i.e. WT presentation itself can be the discriminator; the four TIL TCRs (TCR9a/9b/9c, TCR10) bound with nM to low-uM affinities, and affinity inversely correlated with in vivo persistence; TCR10 recognized an induced, distinct decamer conformation. URL: https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1921964117
- EpiPredict (Sinclair et al., mAbs 2026): a TCRm-specific ML framework trained on high-throughput kinetic off-target data (X-scan variants plus 339 EpiTox-predicted peptides; 530-peptide dataset) for two MAGE-A4 TCRms; it predicted multiple off-targets with MINIMAL sequence similarity to the target epitope, many validated by T2 binding - demonstrating that sequence-similarity, motif and structural-model screens systematically miss off-targets. URL: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/19420862.2025.2601360
- X-scan platform (mAbs 2026, Alloy/ATX): whole-proteome TCRm off-target screening; WT1(126-134)/HLA-A*02:01 TCRms from transgenic mice; ~200 single-chain pHLA phages with single-amino-acid substitutions, NGS plus proteome motif scan; the specificity panel explicitly includes a NO-PEPTIDE control (b2m + alpha chain) and none of the leads bound it; antibodies contacting 4-5 peptide positions were the most specific. URL: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/19420862.2026.2731282

## 6. The affinity-versus-specificity trade-off: numbers, windows and the 'affinity ceiling'

- The 'affinity ceiling' is not structural: Holler et al., PNAS 2000 raised a TCR affinity >100-fold to KD ~9 nM by yeast display with retained peptide specificity, and the accompanying commentary (Foote and Eisen, 'Breaking the affinity ceiling for antibodies and T cell receptors', PNAS 2000) argued there is no inherent limit. URLs: https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.080078297 and https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.97.20.10679
- But high-affinity TCRs can lose self/non-self discrimination: Holler et al., Nat Immunol 2003 isolated high-affinity 2C TCR variants (fast on-rates, reduced CDR flexibility) that cross-reacted with other pMHCs and were stimulated by endogenous self-pMHC - consistent with negative selection maintaining a low-affinity, flexible repertoire. URL: https://www.nature.com/articles/ni863
- The classic quantitative ladder (Zhao et al., J Immunol 2007; 1G4 anti-NY-ESO-1 TCR variants from phage display): affinities 4 uM and 450 nM retained antigen-specific recognition; 85 nM and 5 nM were specific only when CD8 was absent/blocked; the highest-affinity variant (KD 26 pM) COMPLETELY lost antigen specificity in T cells. URL: https://academic.oup.com/jimmunol/article-abstract/179/9/5845/8022410
- Platform-side claim: directed evolution of human TCRs achieved up to 2.5 nM (tax/HLA-A2) and 26 pM (NY-ESO-1/HLA-A2) affinities (Li et al., Nat Biotechnol 2005), and CDR2 engineering was reported to 'dramatically enhance affinity ... without increasing apparent cross-reactivity' (Dunn et al., Protein Sci 2006). URLs: https://www.nature.com/articles/nbt1070 and https://doi.org/10.1110/ps.051936406
- Counter-experiment (velcro): Gee et al., PNAS 2018 added a ~10-fold 'peptide velcro' interaction and showed that the peptide cross-reactivity PROFILES of low-affinity (KD > 100 uM) and higher-affinity (~uM) TCRs are 'remarkably similar' - i.e. affinity per se does not automatically broaden cross-reactivity; the specificity set is largely set by the interface geometry. URL: https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1802746115
- ImmTAC affinity modulation (Robertson et al., Clin Exp Immunol 2024) - the recommended 'window' logic for soluble engagers: (a) combining a strong-affinity TCR with an INTERMEDIATE-affinity anti-CD3 gave optimal activation; strong affinity on BOTH arms reduced maximum cytokine release; (b) an NY-BR-1 TCR (KD 370 pM) panel showed that weakening TCR affinity (down to ~87 nM) increased maximal cytokine release; (c) a very-high-affinity TCR-X (KD 76 pM, t1/2 325 min) cross-reacted with UNPULSED T2 cells and antigen-negative cells (attributed to very weak binding to empty HLA), and lowering affinity improved selectivity; (d) the MAGE-A3 clinical TCR (MAG-IC3; target KD 17 nM) cross-reacted with the titin mimetic at 183 nM (t1/2 0.46 min) - only a ~10x window - and the trial was discontinued after fatal cardiac toxicity. Conclusion: the CD3 arm affinity can be tuned to maximise the difference between on-target and off-target engagement; absolute affinity alone is the wrong metric. URL: https://academic.oup.com/cei/article/215/2/105/7335760
- Mutation-specific counter-example (window can WIDEN with affinity): the KRAS G12D/HLA-A*11:01 JDI TCR (Section 0) went from 63 uM/undetectable WT to 64 pM with a >6000-fold window, because every maturation round included WT depletion and the mutant-specific electrostatic network scaled with affinity. URL: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-022-32811-1
- Format matters: for the same H2 anti-p53R175H binder, increasing affinity improved soluble bispecific killing (EC50 4.2 vs 31 pM) but REDUCED CAR/STAR T-cell function ('Goldilocks'); the optimum affinity cannot be predicted across formats. URL: https://www.jci.org/articles/view/192885
- Review framing: 'The intersection of affinity and specificity in the development and optimization of TCR based therapeutics' (Semin Cell Dev Biol 2018) - TCRs must cross-react (tens of millions of receptors vs a vastly larger peptide universe); clinical losses of specificity with higher-affinity TCRs are documented; T cells have affinity thresholds/filters that TCRm and ImmTAC formats bypass or shift. URL: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.semcdb.2017.10.017
- Immunocore's side-by-side comparison of formats (Holland et al., J Clin Invest 2020): affinity-enhanced TCRs engage pHLA with a broad, balanced energetic footprint across several HLA and peptide side chains and retain greater pHLA selectivity in cellular assays; TCR-mimic ANTIBODIES tend to focus on hot spots on the HLA surface and show greater cross-reactivity. This is the cleanest published statement of why TCRm antibodies are the more cross-reactivity-prone modality. URL: https://www.jci.org/articles/view/130562

## 7. Failure-mode catalogue: when mutation-specific binders broke on WT or peptide-free counter-targets

1. MAGE-A3 (HLA-A*01) affinity-enhanced TCR (a3a) cross-reacted with the muscle protein titin peptide ESDPIVAQY (motif EXDPIXXXY; SPR KD ~100 uM for the off-target). Two patients died of cardiogenic shock; preclinical testing (including HLA-A1 transgenic mice) missed it because the mouse titin peptide is not recognized. Cameron et al., Sci Transl Med 2013: https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/scitranslmed.3006034 ; Linette et al., Blood 2013 (patient course, iPSC-cardiomyocyte assay): https://doi.org/10.1182/blood-2013-03-490565
2. The MAGE-A3/HLA-A*02 TCR trial: neurological toxicity (necrotizing leukoencephalopathy, 2 deaths of 9 patients) from cross-reactivity with MAGE-A12 expressed in brain - sequence-related, not the target. Morgan et al., J Immunother 2013: https://doi.org/10.1097/cji.0b013e3182829903
3. MAG-IC3 (the MAGE-A3 ImmTAC-lineage TCR, target KD 17 nM) - cross-reactive titin mimetic at 183 nM (~10x window) and clinically discontinued; fast off-rate did not protect. Robertson et al. 2024: https://academic.oup.com/cei/article/215/2/105/7335760
4. Very-high-affinity TCR-X (76 pM) bound unpulsed T2 cells and antigen-negative cells - the peptide-free/empty-HLA failure mode; lowering affinity restored selectivity. Same URL.
5. ESK1 (WT1 TCRm): predicted off-targets (MED13L RMFPTPPSL, PIGQ RMFPGEVAL) were confirmed binders; a subset of WT1-negative healthy donor B cells was bound; the tissue interactome found 16 liver peptides with a P1 R/K/A motif, and ESK1-mediated liver spheroid killing was demonstrated; ESK1 never entered clinical development for off-target reasons. URLs: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmb.2015.12.002 and https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-47062-5
6. C8K10D early clones: full-length IgGs at ~20 nM bound KRAS-pulsed cells but also control-peptide-pulsed and completely UNPULSED cells (i.e. peptide-free or irrelevant pMHC) - a defect invisible in pure target-binding assays; fixed only by cell-based negative selection (unpulsed + control-peptide) during a CDR-L2 library campaign. Mol Ther 2026: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ymthe.2026.05.032
7. MAGE-A4 TCRm antibodies: ML analysis found and validated off-targets with minimal sequence similarity to the epitope - sequence/motif screens would have missed them. mAbs 2026: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/19420862.2025.2601360
8. 1G4 anti-NY-ESO-1 TCR at 26 pM: complete loss of target-cell specificity in T cells; 5-85 nM variants required CD8 blockade to show specificity. J Immunol 2007: https://academic.oup.com/jimmunol/article-abstract/179/9/5845/8022410
9. High-affinity 2C TCR variants: self-pMHC reactivity. Nat Immunol 2003: https://www.nature.com/articles/ni863
10. Control (no failure found): H2 (p53 R175H) - no cross-reactive human peptide identified by motif search + T2/COS-7 testing; CLSP-1025 - no binding to WT pMHC up to 400 nM and clean 264-allele class I screen. URLs: https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.abc8697 and https://aacrjournals.org/clincancerres/article/doi/10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-26-1252
11. Root-cause taxonomy that emerges: (a) HLA-helix-dominated epitopes (TCRm antibodies; ESK1 cation-pi to HLA Arg108/169/170) -> cross-reactivity with other peptides on the same HLA; (b) narrow on/off-target windows after affinity maturation with inadequate negative selection (MAG-IC3 17/183 nM; a3a ~100 uM titin); (c) peptide-free/empty-HLA binding at very high affinity (TCR-X; C8K10D parental clones); (d) backbone/conformation-shared mimics (titin vs MAGE-A3 explains the structural basis); (e) format amplification (CD3 arm strength; CAR vs soluble).

## What this implies for Challenge 5 (synthesis)

- The only published binder family against the exact challenge epitope (VVVGADGVGK / HLA-A*11:01) discriminates G12D vs WT largely through the D6 side chain flipping into the HLA F-pocket on engagement (induced fit) plus long-range electrostatics - not through a large set of direct H-bonds to D6. A designed binder should therefore aim to (a) contact D6 or the p6 region directly where possible (D6 is the central bulge in the unbound state and is solvent-exposed), and/or (b) exploit the electrostatically altered pMHC surface created by D6 (the JDI solution), and/or (c) read the p6-dependent peptide conformation/geometry (the C8K10D-style indirect readout).
- Expect the p6-down (F-pocket) conformation to be the high-affinity state for TCR-like binders; designs conditioned only on the unbound (p6-up) AlphaFold model may be targeting a conformation that a natural-like binder would not select (AlphaFold2 also failed to reproduce the C8K10D interface in the Mol Ther study).
- Counter-target discipline: include WT VVVGAGGVGK pMHC, a panel of G12X variants (V/C/R), the peptide-free HLA-A*11:01/b2m, and unrelated HLA-A*11 self-peptides in the very first specificity round; the C8K10D programme shows peptide-free binding appears exactly in the mid-affinity clones and is fixed only by cell-based negative selection.
- Specificity window thinking: aim for a large KD(WT)/KD(G12D) ratio rather than a single affinity target; the JDI case shows the window can be improved during maturation when each round depletes WT binders; the MAG-IC3 case (17 vs 183 nM) shows a ~10x window is fatal clinically.

## Gaps / unverified items

- No public structure of the tebentafusp TCR domain with gp100280-288/HLA-A*02:01 was found (searched Europe PMC and PDB) - its exact contacts cannot be quoted; only the parental-platform and gp100 TCR structural work is public. UNVERIFIED.
- AACR 2026 abstract 6929 (NDV-ADC01-exatecan) is abstract-only: no affinity, epitope geometry, or counter-screen data disclosed. UNVERIFIED beyond the abstract text.
- No dedicated cross-reactivity dataset for the NY-ESO-1 TCRm 3M4E5 itself was found; contacts and affinity maturation are documented, but its off-target profile is not published in the sources reviewed. UNVERIFIED.
- C8K10D crystal structure is 4.5 A; several side-chain-level statements (and all C8K10D5-18 contacts) are inferred/model-supported. Treat interface details as medium confidence.
- ESK1: the specific peptide(s) that caused clinical-grade off-target toxicity are not pinned to a single sequence; the tissue interactome lists 16 candidates (e.g. ILF2 KILPTLEAV) with confirmed spheroid killing but no in-vivo attribution.
- The ADAPT preprint (10.1101/2025.11.19.689381) was peer-review-pending at the time of writing; per-target success rates and full specificity data are only partially reported (it explicitly notes off-target binding to A02-TLM).
- No published design effort exploits the D6-down induced-fit conformation directly in a DESIGNED (non-natural-derived) binder - an open opportunity for Challenge 5.
- Affinity-window recommendations are format-specific (ImmTAC, CAR, IgG/ADC); no universal numeric window for TCRm antibodies exists in the literature reviewed.
