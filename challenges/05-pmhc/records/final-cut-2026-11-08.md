# Final cut — the submitted set is nineteen designs (2026-11-08)

Two steps, same day. First the organizer-aligned ipSAE gate
(`peptide_read >= 0.61`, mean peptide ipSAE over the 5 models, pre-registered
in `notes/evaluation-protocol.md`) was applied to the 20-design set: 16 passed,
4 failed. Then three of the four were restored after their reads were audited.
Only one design left the set.

## Dropped (1)

| design | peptide_read (G12D) | WT | separation | score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `evonim-wave3__run-013__design_0003__g12d-E23K` | 0.545 | 0.313 | 0.232 | 79.5 |

Below the gate **and** the weakest separation of the four below-gate entries:
it reads the wild-type complex at 0.313, so it fails the gate on the axis that
matters most (objective 1, mutation specificity). Dropped on both counts.

## Restored after audit (3)

| design | peptide_read | WT | separation | score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `evorepair-wave2-pep__run-016__design_0005__g12d-L58K-Q15K` | 0.554 | 0.000 | 0.554 | 90.5 (rank 1) |
| `wave2-pep__run-049__design_0002__g12d` | 0.596 | 0.053 | 0.543 | 65.6 |
| `wave2-pep__run-054__design_0005__g12d` | 0.463 | 0.019 | 0.444 | 61.5 |

Why they are back:

- **The gate is the weakest instrument in the set.** The 2026-10-30 metric
  benchmark (400 measured designs) puts ipSAE last at 52.5–72.5 % top-decile
  precision against i_pDAE's 72.5–80 %. `notes/evaluation-protocol.md` keeps
  ipSAE as the organizer-aligned *record* and ranks on the strong metrics.
  Dropping a design on ipSAE alone contradicts the protocol's own ordering.
- **Their reads reproduce, and they are near-misses, not failures.** L58K-Q15K
  was folded twice in independent batches: `evo/gen1` mean 0.574 (per-model
  0.530–0.644) and the clean panel mean 0.554 (0.519–0.598). It misses by
  0.056 on the mean while its best model reads 0.644, above the gate. None of
  the three was affected by the panel's 60-residue truncation bug.
- **They are strong on the instruments that passed their controls.**
  L58K-Q15K: iptm_pep 0.954–0.956, ipdae_pep 0.663–0.684, peptide_share 0.27
  (clears the 0.20 footprint rule), p6_min_dist 6.56 Å (inside the 6–8 Å
  electrostatic shell), E −3.65, three-seed median 0.9867, chai 0.844.
- **Their specificity is the best in the project** — objectives 1 and 2.
  L58K-Q15K on the wild-type complex: read 0.000, iptm_pep 0.268, p6 distance
  32 Å; it does not approach the peptide. dWT +0.747 and dfree +0.766 are
  project records.
- The organizers allow up to 20 designs, so keeping them costs nothing.

Audit source: every recorded read for these designs under `~/c5-work`
(`evo/gen1/`, `evo/gen1/tristate/`, `wt-panel/`), per model and per batch.
No new folds were run — a systematic bias in the Boltz fold pipeline itself
would not show up in this audit.
