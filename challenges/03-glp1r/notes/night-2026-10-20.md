Findings:

- All eight fold confidently (pTM 0.93-0.96) and bury 67-77 A^2 of surface
  on binding — every design is a plausible complex by BoltzGen's own metrics.
- **The ranking differs from ours**: BoltzGen prefers r2a (ipTM 0.844), our
  ipSAE picked r2c (0.727 by ipTM, rank 5). The two metrics weigh the
  interface differently; neither is ground truth. The submission keeps all
  eight, so both views are represented.
- **Zero PLIP H-bonds on every design** — the mce-14 family binds through a
  hydrophobic helix-bundle interface, consistent with the design intent.
