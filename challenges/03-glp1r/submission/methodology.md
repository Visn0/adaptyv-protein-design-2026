# Methodology — GLP1R activating binders

**Hypothesis.** A small binder can do both jobs the brief ranks: hold human GLP1R and switch it on. Class-B GPCR activation splits the job in two — the extracellular groove is the address, and the signal comes from the message region reaching the transmembrane pocket at the depth the hormone itself inserts. The organizers recommend the groove as the epitope; our reading of the published mechanism was that the groove alone is the address half, and that an agonist has to reproduce the whole contact pattern of the natural hormone: the groove contacts, the path into the transmembrane bundle, and the message-region contacts at the hormone's own depth. We explored in bulk before committing — many generators, many backbones, several lengths and modalities — and let the measurements make the calls. Junction-only targeting beat groove targeting. The 15-residue peptide class beat shorter and longer ones. And once a scorer could see the activating geometry, that became the lens we rank by.

**How the designs were made.** Five independent generators, each a different bet on the same question, over twelve campaigns and 788 folded designs. RFdiffusion3 mini-proteins against the receptor's ECD–TMD junction. EvoBind peptides designed from sequence alone, no template. BoltzGen peptides aimed at the junction. BindCraft2 peptides with forced targeting. And RFD4-Proteina — the strongest published generator, run on NVIDIA's free hosted GPU pool — in three rounds that between them produced 401 designs and 48 with the full activating geometry: 256 aimed at the deep message region (27 full-geometry), 73 conditioned on the ECD-to-transmembrane junction (15 of 73, the best full-geometry rate of any generator configuration we ran), and 72 in a shorter 40-to-70-residue band (6 full-geometry, and the best two-domain binding rate of the effort at 13 of 72). Interface-pinned SolubleMPNN re-sequencing was applied to the strongest backbones in three passes (40, 33 and 81 variants); the pass over the RFD4 full-geometry backbones returned 25 full-geometry variants from 81 — a 31 % rate, the highest of any single pass, generator rounds included — and four of the twenty submitted designs are such variants. Seventeen of the twenty come from the three RFD4-Proteina rounds: eight from the deep message-region round, seven conditioned on the ECD-to-transmembrane junction and two from the shorter 40-to-70-residue band. The other three are one RFdiffusion3 mini-protein, one EvoBind peptide and one BoltzGen junction peptide — six method families in all.

**Validation.** Every candidate is re-folded with Boltz-2 2.2.1 against the full 409-residue receptor construct — never a cut-down fragment — five diffusion samples each, seed 42, receptor MSA depth-capped at 1024 rows. Each sample is scored with ipSAE and pDockQ2 at the interface, checked for the intended contact pattern (groove plus the transmembrane-side path), and screened for expression risk (fold confidence and hydropathy). The activating geometry is measured by our activation-geometry gate: six message-region contacts plus the insertion depth below the vestibule rim, with the cutoffs fixed in advance. Two figures are reported per design and they measure different things — the contact count is taken from the best of the five samples, and the agreement figure (`4/5`, `5/5`) is the number of samples that insert at all, our declared 30 Å depth threshold. A design at `6/6 d19.2 5/5` therefore means: the best sample reproduced all six contacts at 19.2 Å, and all five samples inserted. We calibrated the gate on four experimental structures before trusting it: GLP-1 in 6X18 scores 6/6 at the depth it inserts, exendin-4 (7LLL) and the G-protein-biased exendin-P5 (6B3J) reach the pocket but miss contacts, and the weak GLP-1(9-36) metabolite (9IVG) falls short on both counts. A design's geometry score therefore means the same thing the lab's agonists and partial agonists mean. A second, deliberately harsh lens measured how much of the natural hormone's whole 19-contact footprint each design reproduces; it exposed designs that reach the pattern only by wrapping around the receptor rather than inserting (depths of 57 to 74 Å), and those were excluded.

**Scoring: how the twenty were ranked and chosen.** Row order is the ranking, and it is a four-part composite scored in whole and half points, weighted so that the hardest goal dominates: `total = 2·A + 1.5·B + 1·E + 0.5·R`. **A** is activation evidence — a full 6/6 pattern scores 5 with strong insertion agreement, less with weaker agreement, 3.5 for 5/6, and a wrapping pose beyond 30 Å loses a point. **B** is binding, banded on ipSAE with a bonus for pDockQ2 ≥ 0.30. **E** is expression risk, the mean of a pLDDT band and a Kyte-Doolittle hydropathy band. **R** is reach, the fraction of groove, transmembrane-pocket and ECL3 contacts recovered. Ties break on insertion agreement, then depth. Selection then takes the ranking straight down: the score decides, and the method family is used only to order designs that draw on it, so diversity never costs a better-scoring design its slot. Seventeen of the twenty carry the full 6/6 activation pattern and three carry 5/6: the composite weights binding and expression alongside activation, so a 5/6 design that binds hard and is likely to express outranks a 6/6 design that does neither. ipSAE across the file runs 0.840 to 0.933 and the normalized score 87.8 to 95.4. The selector applies a hard quality gate before ranking, because the composite under-weights these: no design below 63 pLDDT, none below 0.18 pDockQ2, and no low-complexity sequence (residue entropy below 2.5 bits, a single residue above 45 % of the chain, or a homopolymer run of 6 or more). It removes 486 of the 800 scored designs and leaves 314 eligible. Across the submitted twenty, pLDDT runs 63.4 to 86.2 and pDockQ2 0.186 to 0.471. Six method families are represented, seven designs are two-domain binding HITs, and the insertion depths span 17.6 to 29.3 Å around the hormone's own 20.6 Å. No two designs in the file exceed 90 % sequence identity; near-duplicates are removed during selection.
