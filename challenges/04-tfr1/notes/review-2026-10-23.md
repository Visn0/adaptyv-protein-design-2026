## 2. New measurements, and what they change

### 2.1 The contact ring around each set is not conserved

The central hypothesis in `strategies.md` §3 says: "the epitope is identical in
both species, so cross-reactivity follows from the epitope choice". The hotspot
residues are identical. The surface a binder touches is not. Exposed residues
within 5 Å of a set, by species:

| set | conserved | divergent | divergent residues |
|---|---|---|---|
| E0 | 6 | 4 | 205K→S, 212L→P, 369E→Q, 370S→N |
| E1 | 0 | 7 | 205K→S, 208R→N, 210V→gap, 211Y→D, 212L→P, 227T→E, 229T→S |
| E2 | 0 | 6 | 211Y→D, 212L→P, 285Q→K, 352D→S, 362T→S, 364R→K |
| E12 (proposal) | 2 | 4 | 211Y→D, 212L→P, 227T→E, 229T→S |

Only one divergent residue in these rings survives as a chemistry class:
364R→K (cation to cation). A binder on E1 or E2 touches mouse-divergent surface
at direct-contact distance.

**Change.** Keep E1 and E2, because they remain the best conserved surface at
the apex. Change the text: the designed core is conserved and cross-reactivity
is a bet that the mouse co-fold and selection improve. Do not claim that
cross-reactivity follows from the epitope choice. The X rubric bands stay.

### 2.2 11RN shows what a real binder touches

11RN holds the B19V VP1u RBD (two 62-residue chains) on the TfR1 ectodomain.
The chain A interface:

- direct contacts at 5 Å: 202 I, 203 V, 204 D, 208 R, 209 L, 210 V, 211 Y,
  212 L, 348 N, 371 K. These are the named patch, its flanks, and 348/371.
- the 8 Å envelope adds 199–201, 205, 207, 213–215, 344, 347, 368, 370, 372, 374.
- buried interface area: 916 Å².
- no glycan contact.

The apex carries direct evidence of a real binder. That binder touches the
divergent core of the named patch, not the conserved residues. E1 (through 209)
and the ledge (through 344/347/374) sit inside the 8 Å envelope, not at 5 Å.

**Change.** Campaign 1 task 4 is closed for 11RN. Use the 10-residue contact
list above as the known-good count in the site-map assertion.

### 2.3 The transferrin claim is verified

1SUV holds transferrin on TfR1. Transferrin contacts at 5 Å: 123–126 (the
N-terminal tail) and 527–670 (the protease-like and helical domains). Zero
contacts fall inside the apical window 189–382. The nearest transferrin
contact to each set:

| set | nearest contact | distance |
|---|---|---|
| E0 | 348..624 | 42.7 Å |
| E1 | 374..624 | 42.5 Å |
| E2 | 351..624 | 37.7 Å |

**Change.** The brief's claim is now measured, not repeated. The methodology
cites the number.

### 2.4 A compact dual-anchor set exists (proposal E12)

344 K and 374 K are the two conserved switch anchors. Their distance is 11.5 Å
(min atom) or 13.8 Å (CA-CA). 347 G to 374 K is 15.8 Å, past the 15 Å hotspot
rule, so the bridge set holds only the anchors: **E12 = {344 K, 374 K}**.

E12 helps in one way. A binder that contacts both anchors can put two or three
histidines on two lysines. The switch claim then rests on two sites instead of
one. The two anchors sit on one ledge, and the VP1u 8 Å envelope covers both.

**Change.** Campaign 1 generates four sets, not three: E0, E1, E2, E12, at 24
designs each. The fold queue order is E0, E1, E12, E2. Each set folds before
any set gets a second batch.

## 3. Changes per campaign

### Campaign 1 — triage
Agree with the plan, plus four additions.

1. The fold-memory probe runs first, with 1 sample, then 5 samples of the same
   complex for the wall-time number. Expectation: the 639-residue fold sat at
   the card limit. The C2 reference is 11.3 GiB host for a ~540-token complex.
   A 739-token complex scales near 18 GiB host, and the card has about 9.8 GB
   free. The apical-block fold plus the separate dimer clash check is the
   likely operating point. The measurement decides.
2. Fetch and cache the receptor MSAs (human protomer and mouse), depth-capped
   at 1024 rows, before the first fold.
3. After the cut of the 180–400 block, shift every hotspot number by -179 and
   check the residue letters. A wrong shift is AGENT.md mistake 21.
4. Task 4 is done: 11RN and 1SUV are in `inputs/`, and the numbers are in
   section 2. The 96-generation batch (4 sets x 24) replaces the 72 batch.

### Campaign 2 — binding families
Agree with the plan. Two details change.

1. The RFD3 control arm is the first arm to drop when the fold queue slips.
   The calibration gives it 2.5 %.
2. The "free until 2026-11-04" claim for the NVIDIA RFD4 pool has no source in
   this repo. Verify the window at use time, before the RFD4 arm depends on it.

Unchanged: Genie 3 primary, RFD4 co-primary, full-backbone ProteinMPNN, the
SolubleMPNN fallback, the fold-picks-then-re-sequence order, and the screens.

### Campaign 3 — the switch layer
Agree with the mechanism and the three arms. The position rule changes.

- Old rule: side-chain centroid within 8 Å of the anchor lysine terminal
  nitrogen.
- New rule: a histidine Nδ1 or Nε2 atom within 5.5 Å of the anchor Lys NZ in
  the co-folded pose. Keep the position solvent exposed.
- Never point a histidine at 343 E, 350 E, 360 D, or any Asp/Glu within 6 Å of
  the histidine. 360 D is new: it is conserved, exposed at 32.8 %, and it sits
  near the ledge sets. A histidine against an acid inverts the direction.
- Prefer parent residues that are polar or charged.
- The top band needs two or three histidines. Group them on one anchor, or use
  an E12 family for one per anchor.

Supporting literature: Chugai's recycling antibodies use this direction and
this mechanism (protonated histidine against positively charged epitope
residues: doi.org/10.1038/nbt.1691 and PMC4966519). The antibody F06 binds TfR
with strong pH sensitivity from 7.4 to 5.2 and cross-reacts to mouse
(PMC12456222). A 2026 nanobody set is pH-dependent and cross-reactive across
human, cyno and mouse, with a structural mechanism (bioRxiv 2026.07.29.741483).

### Campaign 4 — mouse cross-fold
Agree. No change. The mouse maps are now recorded (alignment): E1 209/210/212
/376, E2 345/346/349/352/353, E12 346/376.

### Campaign 5 — selection
Agree. The dual-anchor ladder is the strongest declared-control candidate.
All floors live in the selector. The pre-flight assertions stay.

## 5. Campaign 1 — the start list

The card is idle now. `pdt serve` runs on port 8737. Start in this order:

1. **Fold-memory probe.** One dummy complex: a 100-residue binder against the
   639-residue protomer. Run it under `memlimit.py` with `watch_resources.py`.

   1.7 hours at 66 s per design.
