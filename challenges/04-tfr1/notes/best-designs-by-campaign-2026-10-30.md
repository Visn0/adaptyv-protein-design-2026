## Are these numbers good? — the reference points we have

**In-house instrument calibration** (same protocol, measured on this box,
`notes/screen-predictor-options.md`):

| reference | restrained ipTM | note |
|---|---|---|
| native VP1u RBD (a binder known to work) | 0.42-0.48 | floats to 0.19 unrestrained |
| C1's failed set (E0) | 0.21-0.44 | all below the native control |
| measured dynamic range, replicate spread | 0.21-0.75, +-0.02 | the instrument ranks designs |

Reading: the champions at 0.78 sit at the very top of the measured range,
clearly above the natural reference, and the ranking is reproducible. But a
restrained read is directed by construction (the restraints build the
interface) - a consistency read, not independent evidence of binding.

**The only wet-lab calibration we have** (Anthropic's released TNF campaign,
real outcomes; `notes/lessons-c2-c3.md`, sourced from
`02-tnfa/notes/filter-calibration.md`):

- Generator win rates: Genie 3 34.8 % of designs bound, RFdiffusion3 2.5 %,
  RFdiffusion 0 % - the generator dominates; the file here is genie3/RFD4
  lineage.
- ipSAE bands: **0.6-0.7 -> 0 of 22 bound; 0.8 and above -> 25.7 % bound
  (base rate 8.7 %)**. One real binder (2.1 nM, mouse cross-reactive) scored
  0.06 - ipSAE is neither necessary nor sufficient.
- Our twenty under the same conventions: max-over-models ipSAE median
  0.014, mean 0.13, best 0.670 (`b3_E12_r90003`), 19 of 20 below 0.6. On this axis the file
  sits in or near the band that bound nothing in the TNF campaign.

**Other references:** the organizers' prescreen (catches 103/105 known
binders, zero false flags on 80 unrelated designs - 20/20 pass is a real but
weak positive filter); structural novelty (TM <= 0.49 against known-binder
references - no accidental copies, and no free "known to work" evidence
either); the mouse ratio the challenge asks for (near 1: champion 0.688/0.782
= 0.88); PROPKA's +-0.8-1 unit error against our -2.14/-2.82 switch shifts
(direction credible, magnitude model-limited).

**Bottom line:** good as relative evidence (best-of-set, above the native
control, reproducible); not established as an absolute success prediction -
against the one wet-lab-calibrated metric we have, our numbers are below the
band that bound. That asymmetry is why the twenty stays diverse across
families, folds and mechanisms.
