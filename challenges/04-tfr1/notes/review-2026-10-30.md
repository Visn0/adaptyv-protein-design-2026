## Verdict

The selection itself is in good shape and now defensible: re-run from scratch
with the same outcome, generated by one script, and accompanied by an honest
statement of what the metrics do and do not predict. Every open finding above
is an edit to a record or a sentence — none needs a fold. The exposure is
concentrated in the two documents that get read first, the evaluation-coverage
statement and the self-evaluation, and in both cases the file currently claims
slightly more than its own records show.

The pattern worth fixing procedurally, since it is the third round it appears:
each round's evidence records are built, a later round swaps designs into the
file, and the records are not rebuilt. Having the selector regenerate the
coverage records as its last step would close the class, not the instance.
